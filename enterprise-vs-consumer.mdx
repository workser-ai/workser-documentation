**Start with a workforce of your own, then the Team plan shares one task board and AI agents across your people, and we run Enterprise contracts with training.**

## At a glance

| Area | Self-serve plans | Enterprise |
| --- | --- | --- |
| How you start | Sign up on the web; Free needs no card | A rollout agreed with us |
| Plans | Free, Starter $20, Pro $100, Team $20 per person (2 people minimum) | Custom agreement and invoicing |
| AI agents | Workser + 3, 50 or 100; Team 50 per person, shared | Agreed in the contract |
| Credits | 50 to start, then 1,800 or 10,000 a month; Team 1,800 per person | Agreed credit and usage terms |
| Computers at once | 1, 5 or 10; Team 5 per person | Agreed capacity |
| Guarantee | 7-day money-back guarantee on your first plan payment | Defined in the agreement |
| Support | Community, email or priority by plan | Agreed support and training |

See every limit in [Plans and credits](/ai-workforce/plans-and-credits).

## The Team plan

- $20 per person a month, 2 people minimum.
- 1,800 credits a month per person, shared.
- One shared task board and AI agents across your people.
- Skills and connectors set up once for everyone.
- Central billing and admin, and email support.

Members get one of four roles in the organization: `owner`, `admin`, `member` or `viewer`. Only an owner or admin can manage billing, remove members or ask for the money-back guarantee.

## Enterprise

Enterprise agreements set capacity, credits, security review, support and training for your organization. SSO, data residency and SLA requirements are confirmed in the agreement. Check [Compliance status](/security/compliance-status) for what is certified today.
