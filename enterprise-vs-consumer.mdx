Workser Code and the wider Workser platform serve individuals, small teams and larger organizations. **Spark, Starter and Scale** are the current self-service plans. **Enterprise** is a tailored agreement for organizations with specific infrastructure, access, support or procurement requirements.

## At a glance

| Area | Self-service | Enterprise |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Onboarding | Free desktop download and a new organization's 7-day Spark trial | Sales-assisted rollout as agreed |
| Subscription | Spark $10, Starter $25, Scale $100 per month | Custom agreement and invoicing |
| Projects | 2, 4 or 20 by plan | Unlimited in the published offer |
| Cloud | Published database, files and transfer allowances | Custom cloud allowance and infrastructure terms |
| Database | Dedicated Postgres database per paid project | Dedicated infrastructure scoped in the agreement |
| App user authentication | Unlimited app users | Requirements defined in the agreement |
| Workspace team | 1, up to 3 or up to 10 members | Agreed organization access and governance |
| Credits | Purchased credits; monthly grant on Starter and Scale | Negotiated credit and usage terms |
| Support | Community, priority email or priority support by plan | Named customer success manager and agreed support |

SSO, data residency and SLA requirements should be confirmed in the Enterprise agreement. Their appearance in a plan offer is not proof of certification or that every control is active for your organization. Consult [Compliance status](/security/compliance-status) and [Enterprise network requirements](/security/enterprise-network-requirements).
