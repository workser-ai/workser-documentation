---
title: "Enterprise vs. Consumer"
description: "Same two products, different depth of governance, support, and data controls."
---

Workser Business and Workser Desktop are the same products for every customer — there's no separate enterprise codebase. What changes across tiers is tenant isolation, access governance, and the compliance artifacts available to you.

## At a glance

| | Consumer / self-serve | Enterprise |
|---|---|---|
| Onboarding | Self-serve signup, credit card | Sales-assisted, contract + DPA, security review |
| Data isolation | Free tier: shared database | Paid tier (Spark+): dedicated database and storage per project |
| Access control | Single org, informal roles | Multi-seat org with `owner / admin / member / viewer` roles enforced |
| Compliance artifacts | Not typically needed | DPA, Security Whitepaper, Subprocessor List — see [Security & Trust](/security/whitepaper) |
| Billing | Self-serve credit top-up | Per-user credit limits, invoiced billing history |
| Support | Docs + community | Direct support channel, incident notification per your DPA |
