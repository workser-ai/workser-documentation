## Plans

Workser Business is subscription-based, with tiers from Free up through Starter, Launch/Pro, and Expert — each with its own monthly AI credit allowance, project count, database/storage limits, and workflow execution limits. Check your dashboard's pricing page for current figures, since limits can change independently of this guide.

## How credits work

Workser uses a credit/wallet model at the organization level:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Credit balance" icon="wallet">Available AI credits your organization can spend.</Card>
  <Card title="Auto-topup" icon="rotate">Set a threshold below which Workser automatically purchases more credits.</Card>
  <Card title="One-time top-ups" icon="plus">Manual purchases whenever you need more.</Card>
  <Card title="Credit ledger" icon="list">Every refill, top-up, deduction, and adjustment is recorded with a running balance — fully auditable.</Card>
</CardGroup>

If you're on an enterprise organization, admins can also set **per-user credit limits** to cap how much an individual member can spend.

## What consumes credits

AI-driven actions consume credits: building or iterating on a web app, running an AI Employee task, and AI-evaluated workflow triggers. Plain workflow triggers (webhook, schedule, manual) and simple deterministic nodes (HTTP request, transform, logic) don't consume credits.

## Invoices and billing history

Paid invoices and receipts are tracked separately from your live credit ledger, viewable per organization — useful for reconciling spend against your accounting.

## What we store

We don't store your raw card number — our payment processor handles that. We hold a customer ID, subscription status, and the transaction records needed to manage your plan and credits. See our [Privacy Policy](/security/privacy-policy) for the full picture.
