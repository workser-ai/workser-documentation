Workser bills at the **organization** level. Keep three costs separate when planning your budget:

1. **Workser subscription:** workspace access and included cloud resources.
2. **Model usage:** either your connected coding-agent provider or Workser credits for the built-in agent and applicable hosted services.
3. **Additional cloud usage:** usage above the included allowance where metered billing is enabled.

## Current plans

New organizations receive **7 days of Spark free with no card required**. Free accounts and existing Launch subscriptions are not additional current self-service offers. Existing plans may retain different terms; inspect your organization's Billing view.

For database, file storage, egress, domains, authentication, team size, backups and overages, see the [full cloud comparison](/code/pricing#compare-plans). That guide is the detailed documentation reference for current published allowances.

## What consumes credits

- **Connected coding agents:** model usage is billed by the provider configured in the CLI. Those calls do not spend Workser Code credits.
- **Built-in Workser Code agent:** paid model usage draws from the organization's credit balance. Actual model cost, plus the current 15% uplift, converts at 100 credits per dollar. It is not a fixed charge per step.
- **Agent Cloud and other hosted AI:** have their own model and runtime usage rules. Do not apply the local coding agent's example to every service.

Spark has no monthly credit grant. It can still use the built-in agent with purchased credits. A free cloud trial does not make paid model calls free. See [Workser Code credits](/code/pricing#workser-code-credits).

## Top-ups and the ledger

Use Billing to view the organization's available credits and transaction ledger. One-time top-ups add credits when needed. If auto-topup is available and you enable it, review its trigger and purchase amount before saving.

The ledger records refills, purchases, deductions and adjustments. A pre-run balance check is not a final cost estimate: actual usage may be settled after a run. Enterprise credit limits and invoicing follow the organization's agreement.

## Cloud overage

Published rates above the included allowance are **$1.25/GB for database storage**, **$0.15/GB for files**, and **$0.50/GB for egress**. For example, 10 GB of extra egress is $5. Database and file allowances are pooled across the organization's projects.

Check which meters are enabled for your organization before assuming extra usage is automatically billed. Project, app and team member limits require a plan upgrade. [Cloud overage details](/code/pricing#cloud-overage).

## Trial expiry and plan changes

When a trial expires without a paid subscription, paid-tier features can pause. Expiry does not itself delete your project or data. Review Billing for the access available to your organization and contact support if the displayed allowance differs from your plan. Do not treat a trial as a long-term backup or retention service.

## Invoices and payment data

Invoices and receipts are separate from the live credit ledger. Use the organization's billing history to reconcile subscription payments, top-ups and applicable metered usage.

Workser's payment processor handles card details. Workser stores the customer identifier, subscription status and transaction records needed to manage billing. See the [Privacy Policy](/security/privacy-policy).
