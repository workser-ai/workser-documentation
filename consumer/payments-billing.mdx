## Plans

Workser is subscription-based, with tiers from Free up through Spark, Starter, Launch, and Scale, plus a negotiated Enterprise tier — each with its own monthly AI credit allowance, project count, database/storage limits, and workflow execution limits. Check your dashboard's pricing page for current figures, since limits can change independently of this guide.

| Plan | Price | Workser AI credits | Projects | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | — | Shared, limited | Good for trying Workser Business before subscribing |
| **Spark** | $10/mo | **None included** | 2 | Built around your own local AI agent via [Orbit](/orbit/overview) — see below |
| Starter | $25/mo | 1,500/mo | 3 | Adds Workser-run AI agents on top |
| Launch | $50/mo | 3,500/mo | 5 | For production operators |
| Scale | $100/mo | 6,500/mo | 10 | Heavier workflow/database limits |
| Enterprise | Custom | Custom, volume pricing | Unlimited | SSO, dedicated infra, data residency, SLA — [contact sales](https://workser.ai/consulting) |

<Tip>
**Every new organization starts with 7 days of Spark, free — no card required.** If you haven't subscribed by the time the trial ends, paid-tier features (including [Orbit](/orbit/overview)) pause until you do; your project and its data are kept, not deleted.
</Tip>

### Why Spark includes no Workser AI credits

Spark is priced around **bring-your-own-key**: on Workser Desktop, [Orbit](/orbit/overview) connects a coding-agent CLI you already pay for (Claude Code, Codex, or OpenCode) to Workser's deploy/hosting infrastructure. Code generation runs on *your* agent subscription, not Workser's — so Spark's $10/month covers the hosting side only (a dedicated database, storage, auth, and deploys), not AI inference. If you also want to use Workser's own hosted AI agents — AI Employees, workflow automation, or Computer Use — Starter and above add monthly Workser AI credits for that.

## How credits work

Workser uses a credit/wallet model at the organization level:

  <Card title="Credit balance" icon="wallet">Available AI credits your organization can spend.</Card>
  <Card title="Auto-topup" icon="rotate">Set a threshold below which Workser automatically purchases more credits.</Card>
  <Card title="One-time top-ups" icon="plus">Manual purchases whenever you need more.</Card>
  <Card title="Credit ledger" icon="list">Every refill, top-up, deduction, and adjustment is recorded with a running balance — fully auditable.</Card>

If you're on an enterprise organization, admins can also set **per-user credit limits** to cap how much an individual member can spend.

## What consumes credits

AI-driven actions consume credits: building or iterating on a web app, running an AI Employee task, and AI-evaluated workflow triggers. Plain workflow triggers (webhook, schedule, manual) and simple deterministic nodes (HTTP request, transform, logic) don't consume credits. **Orbit is the exception that proves the rule**: because code generation runs on your own connected coding agent, not Workser's, using Orbit to build and deploy doesn't consume Workser AI credits at all — only your Spark-or-above subscription for the hosting side.

## Invoices and billing history

Paid invoices and receipts are tracked separately from your live credit ledger, viewable per organization — useful for reconciling spend against your accounting.

## What we store

We don't store your raw card number — our payment processor handles that. We hold a customer ID, subscription status, and the transaction records needed to manage your plan and credits. See our [Privacy Policy](/security/privacy-policy) for the full picture.
