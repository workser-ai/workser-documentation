## What it is

A structured place to describe your business so every AI agent you configure reasons about the same real context, instead of you re-explaining it in every conversation. Records are organized into four types:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Company" icon="building">Industry, size, location, and core identity.</Card>
  <Card title="Market" icon="chart-line">The market(s) you operate in.</Card>
  <Card title="Customer" icon="users">Target customer/persona profiles, including pain points.</Card>
  <Card title="Strategy" icon="compass">Vision, mission, and strategic direction.</Card>
</CardGroup>

## Why it matters

Without this, an AI Employee or workflow AI step has to infer your business context from whatever's in the current conversation. With it, a marketing agent and a support agent both reason about the same company, market, and customer — without you repeating yourself.

## How agents use it

[AI Employees](/workser-business/ai-employees) and AI-driven workflow steps read from your organization's Business Data Platform records as part of their available context, alongside whatever files or connected-app data you've given them for a specific task.

## Getting the most out of it

- Keep customer/persona records specific — detailed pain points and target-market info noticeably improve how well an agent reasons about your customers, versus a generic description.
- Update strategy records when your positioning changes, so agents aren't reasoning from stale context.

## Data handling

Business Data Platform records are treated as your data under Workser's [Privacy Policy](/security/privacy-policy): not used to train models without your written consent, encrypted per Workser's standard controls, and deleted along with your project or organization data when you delete it.
