**A front desk is one business address, number or chat app with the whole team behind it.** The AI agent that answers passes
each request to the teammate it fits; that teammate replies from the same address, number or chat, and the work behind the
reply goes to whoever owns it.

The page is **Front desk** in the sidebar, after Home and Tasks.

## What the page shows

- **Today** or **This week**, and a headline like **"Today: 11 of 14 contacts handled · 1 in progress · 2 need you"**.
- **All**, **Needs you**, **Working** and **Done**, each with a count.
- One row per contact: channel icon, who wrote, what it was, the outcome, up to three background lines, the person who has
  it, and the status.

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Done** | Answered and finished |
| **Working** | Being handled now |
| **Needs you** | A person has to decide or answer |
| **Not answered** | It arrived and nobody has picked it up yet; counted under **Needs you** |
| **Screened** | Kept without a Task because it needed no work (a newsletter, an automated notice); it is counted, but the filters do not list it today |

Rows with a Task behind them open it. Empty filters read "Nothing in yet", "Nothing needs you", "Nothing in progress" or
"Nothing done yet", with a note that everything appears once the business has an address or number.

Home shows one quiet line when something arrived today: **"Front desk today · 14 contacts · 12 handled · 2 need you"**.

## Set it up

With no line yet, the page offers three actions:

- **Give an email**
- **Get a phone number**
- **Connect a chat app**

The setup note explains the split: "Your Front Desk Team answers them: Workser Support replies and picks up, Workser Sales
takes quotes and new leads, and the rest of your AI agents do the work behind each reply."

Open a line for Workser Support, or connect a chat app, to start.

## Who answers

Lines are set in **Settings › Identity › Lines** (also reachable from a line card's **Open Lines**):

| Setting | What it decides |
| --- | --- |
| **Who answers** | The AI agent that takes the first reply or call. Default: **Workser, the manager**. |
| **Unmatched sign-up codes** | Who receives sign-up codes nobody asked for. Default: **Who answers, then Workser**. Email and phone only. |
| **Team behind this line** | The teammates who can take the work on. Free; add as many as you like. |

The team line changes by channel: "Who answers passes each email on; the teammate replies from this address." / phone:
"takes the call and passes it on; the teammate calls back from this number." / chat: "takes each message and passes it on;
the teammate replies in the same chat."

A line card reads **"\<answeredBy\> answers, with \<A\> and \<B\> behind it"**, and either **"How we answer: set"** or
**"Set how it answers"**.

## How we answer

Each line has one guide that everyone answering it follows, whoever picks up. Empty fields mean the AI agent's own way.

| Field | What it sets |
| --- | --- |
| **How we sound** | Tone, for example "Warm and short. First names. No emoji." |
| **Always** | What every reply should do: offer pickup times, give the order number |
| **Never** | What no reply may do: promise a delivery date, offer a discount |
| **Ask me first when** | The cases that wait for your yes. On email and chat you see the reply first; on a call the caller hears that someone will get back to them |
| **Sign-off** | How every written reply ends (email) |
| **Business hours** | The hours the line says the team is back; messages are still answered |

Limits are short by design: 300 characters for tone, 800 for Always and Never, 500 for Ask-me, 120 for the sign-off.

## What the team shares

Everything that arrives is in the same page, with who answered and who did the work, so a hand-off does not create a second
inbox. The reply keeps the same address, number or chat; the AI agent never says it is somebody else.

## If something is wrong

| Symptom | Likely cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The page says "Nothing in yet" | No address, number or chat app is connected | Use the three setup buttons |
| A contact is **Not answered** | Nobody has picked it up | Answer it, or give the line a team |
| A contact is **Needs you** | It waits for a decision | Open it from **Needs you** |
| A reply does not follow the guide | The guide is empty, or the line has none | **Settings › Identity › Lines → How we answer** |
| The wrong AI agent answers | **Who answers** is set to another agent | Change it in Lines |
| The guide says "Coming with the next update" | This server has an older core | The line answers in each AI agent's own way until it updates |
