**In Workser, an owner or admin connects a card once per project in Settings › Card and spending, then sets each AI agent's
monthly limit and approves every purchase with their passkey.** Card checkout is on Starter, Pro and Team. Nothing is
bought without you: not by a Task, not by an Automation, not by an AI agent acting on its own.

The card behind this is **Agentcard** (its Vault keeps your card, its Issuing makes single-use cards), which is why the
panel ends with "Powered by agentcard.com".

## Before you start

- **Who can do it:** an owner or admin connects the card, adds it, changes rules, approves orders and disconnects.
  Everyone else sees the card and the orders and the line "An owner or admin connects this and approves orders."
- **Plan:** Starter, Pro or Team. Below Starter the panel says "Card checkout is on Starter and up" with an Upgrade button.
- **What you need:** the card you want your AI agents to use, and a phone number (with country code) or an email address
  to receive a one-time code.
- **Where it lives:** **Settings › Card and spending**. Each AI agent also has a **Cards** view under
  **Resources**, and the project's **Card** tab in the workspace shows the same ground from the work side.

## Connect the project card

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings › Card and spending">
    The panel starts at "Not connected" and shows the four steps: **Send me a code**, **Type the code**,
    **Add your card**, **Connected**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send me a code">
    Enter the email address or phone number (with country code) that should receive the code. It is the code that proves
    the card account is yours, not a Workser password.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Type the code">
    Paste the code back into the panel. If it does not arrive, send a new one; the newest code is the one that works.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your card">
    The card page opens in its own tab. Add the card there and come back. The panel then reads **Connected** and shows the
    card it will use, for example "Uses the project card ending 4242".
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can disconnect at any time. Orders already placed stay in the list.

## Set what each AI agent may buy

Under the card, **Each AI agent's rules** has one row per AI agent. Three settings, nothing else:

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Can buy for you** | Off means this AI agent cannot place an order at all. |
| **Monthly limit (USD)** | The most this AI agent may spend in a calendar month. It resets on the 1st at 00:00 UTC, and purchases still waiting for your passkey count toward it. |
| **Stores it may use** | **Any store**, or **Only these**: the store names exactly as they appear at checkout. |

An AI agent's **Cards** view (Resources › Cards) shows this month's spend against its limit as one thin meter, then the
last six months as small bars, so a limit is not a number you have to remember.

## Approve a purchase

Every purchase waits for the owner's yes. When an order is waiting, it appears under **Orders** with the store, the date
and the amount.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the order">
    **Settings › Card and spending › Orders**, the project **Card** tab, or the AI agent's **Cards** view. A waiting
    order reads **Waiting for your passkey**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve with passkey">
    The passkey page opens in its own tab. Confirm there. The order moves to **Payment pending**, then **Paid**, and
    leaves the waiting list once it is paid.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Or say no">
    **Don't buy** cancels the order before it is placed. It stays in the list as **Cancelled**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

| Order status | What it means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Placing it | The order is being set up at the store | Wait a moment, then Refresh |
| Waiting for your passkey | It is waiting for you | Approve with passkey, or Don't buy |
| Payment pending | The passkey was accepted; the charge is settling | Wait; do not order again |
| Paid | Done | Nothing |
| Declined | The card or the store refused it | Check the card, then try again if you still want it |
| Didn't go through | The order failed | Try again, or ask the AI agent to stop |
| Check this one | Workser could not read the final state | Look at your card statement before ordering again |
| Cancelled | You said no, or it was cancelled | Nothing |

Declined, failed and cancelled orders are never counted as spent.

## Single-use cards

Below the project card, **Single-use cards** are issued to an AI agent for one purchase: a card that exists for one
checkout and one amount.

- Each card waits for your yes before it is **made** and again before it is **used**.
- Cards are funded from a prepaid balance: **Add prepaid funds**, choose an amount from $1 to $10,000, and confirm on
  the funding page that opens. This is a prepaid balance, not Workser credits.
- The first time, you confirm the phone number that receives the issuance code.
- A card has a checkout window. Past it, the row says **checkout window ended**; the card cannot be used again.
- **Close** a card to end it early. Closing is permanent.
- The rows show card issuance, not proof of a completed purchase. An uncertain issuance stays reserved until it is
  reviewed in the card account.

## What it costs

- **The purchase amount goes on the card you added**, through Agentcard. It is not paid from Workser credits.
- **Workser's per-request fee** for asking the card service is **0 today**, while the card service is in its free beta.
  If it changes, the fee is charged in credits, one per request, and shows in **Settings › Credits and usage**.
- **Single-use cards** are paid from the prepaid balance you fund in the same panel, not from the project card.
- Card checkout itself is part of the plan, on Starter, Pro and Team ([plans and credits](/ai-workforce/plans-and-credits)).

## Across your projects

When the business has more than one project, Settings › Card and spending also lists **Across your projects**: each
project's card, this month's spend and how many orders are waiting for a passkey. It is read-only; a project's card and
rules are set in that project.

## If something is wrong

| Symptom | Likely cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "Waiting for your code" never moves | Wrong address, or the code expired | Send a new code and enter it within its window |
| "Needs reconnecting" | The card connection was revoked at the provider | Reconnect and add the card again if asked |
| Approve with passkey does nothing | The browser blocked the new tab, or you are not an owner or admin | Allow the tab, or open the passkey link shown in the panel |
| An order says "Check this one" | The final state could not be read | Check your card statement before ordering again |
| The AI agent cannot buy | **Can buy for you** is off, the limit is used, or the store is not in **Only these** | Change the rule, or wait for the 1st |
| Single-use card stuck at Reserved | Issuance is uncertain | Review it in the card account before issuing another |
