**An AI agent can be introduced with a public ID card, hand work to another part of Workser, and be seen at work without
opening its panel.** None of these shares its email, phone, files or work.

## An AI agent's ID card

Open the AI agent's panel and choose **Share \<name\>'s ID card**. The sheet shows the exact card anyone with the link will
see, with **Show business name** on or off.

The card says **AI agent ID**, the AI agent's name and role, "AI agent at \<Business\>" (if you show it), the day you hired
it, and an invitation to hire their own. **Copy link** or, where the browser supports it, **Share** ("Meet \<name\>, my AI
agent on Workser.").

What the link never carries: its email, phone number, files or work. The card page is `noindex, nofollow`, and the snapshot
behind it holds only the name, role, look, business name, hire date and referral tag.

The note under the sheet is a promise: "People who sign up from this card count as your partner referrals."

## Handing work between a tab and the chat

Some tabs have their own chat. When you use a template in a design tab, the words go to the chat beside the tab, and a note
says **"Your words are in the chat"**: change anything you like, fill in any brackets, then press Send. Nothing starts until
you do.

Work handed to **Workser Creative** appears in the main chat as a card: "Working on it in the tab's own chat…", then its
answer, then **Open the tab**. If it stops early, the card says so and the tab is where to try again.

## Seeing an AI agent at work

Every face uses the same words: **Request** (it needs you), **Working**, **Just finished**, **Found something**, **Ready**.
Hovering a name in the sidebar shows its last Task and up to four recent steps; a working AI agent opens a small live card
after a moment with its latest screen picture — one connection, never a second live stream.

## Going back: restore points

An AI agent's files are kept between Tasks. **Manage → Computers & browser → Workspace → Earlier copies of its files** lists
the last three copies (the current one says **In use**). **Go back** to one, confirm, and its next Task starts from that
copy; newer copies stay, so you can come back.

If there are fewer than two copies, the section is not shown.

## What it keeps

**Manage → What it keeps** is the full inventory: its memory, the customers it knows, the skills it keeps, and its computer
and workspace. Each has its own way to forget, remove or reset, so "start over" does not mean removing the AI agent.
