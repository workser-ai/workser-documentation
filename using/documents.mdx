**Documents are the papers of the business — contracts, invoices, reports, sheets — kept in one tab. The Library is every
file behind them, including images, video, audio and what a Task made.** They are the same files, seen two ways; a Task's
**Result** is what that one Task made.

## Two views of the same files

| Place | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Documents** tab | Documents only: word files, sheets, PDFs, slides and tables. "Contracts, money papers, reports and sheets, kept in one place." |
| **Library** (page) | Every file: "Everything your AI agents made, and the files you shared with them." |
| **Result** | What one Task made, in that Task's Result tab |

The Documents tab guesses what a document is about from its name (then from the Task's title for agent files): **Contracts and
legal**, **Finance**, **Reports**, **People and HR**, **Sales papers**. A poorly named file may land in **All**.

## Documents

The tab has:

- **Requests**: documents waiting on you or overdue, up to six.
- **Start from a template**: service agreement, invoice with a payment link, proposal, and **View all 30**.
- **Your documents**: folders, task sets, and every document.

**New** makes a document, **Upload** adds files, **New folder** groups them (when the server supports folders). A task's files
show as a set: "3 files".

### Status and due dates

Each document can carry one status and a due date:

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Draft** | Being written |
| **Waiting for you** | It needs your read or answer |
| **Sent** | It went out |
| **Signed** | It came back signed |
| **Paid** | The money arrived |
| **Done** | Finished |

A sent or waiting document past its date shows **Overdue**. **Requests** offers **Follow up** (a follow-up Task in the chat) or
**Read it**.

### Find one

Filter by topic (**All**, Contracts and legal, Finance, Reports, People and HR, Sales papers), by type (**Docs**, **Sheets**,
**PDFs**, **Slides**), search, sort (**Newest first**, **Oldest first**, **Name A to Z**, **Name Z to A**, **Type**), and switch
list or grid.

Empty: "No documents yet. Ask in the chat beside this tab, pick a template, or upload the files you already have."

## Upload

- **Documents** accepts `.doc`, `.docx`, `.xls`, `.xlsx`, `.csv`, `.tsv`, `.pdf`, `.ppt`, `.pptx`, `.md`, `.txt`, `.odt`, `.ods`,
  **10 at a time**. The rest says: "Adding the first 10 of 12 files. Upload the rest after these."
- **Library** takes the same types by picker or drag-and-drop, without the 10-file cap.
- **1 GB per file.** Larger: "x is over 1 GB. Pick a smaller file, or share a link to it."
- Success: **"Added. Every AI agent in this project can read it now."**

## Folders and sharing

- **Folders** keep the Documents tab tidy; deleting a folder puts its files back in Documents, it does not delete them.
- **Share** (Library only) makes a public link: "Anyone who has it can open this file, without a Workser account. You can turn
  it off later." **Copy link**, then **Turn off link** stops it for everyone.
- Documents can be renamed; editing an uploaded file saves an **edited copy** beside the original.

## What is not here

- **No trash or restore.** Removing a file is permanent: "Your AI agents stop seeing it, and any link you shared stops
  working. This can't be undone." For a file an AI agent made, it also leaves the Task that made it.
- **No upload of folders**, and attachments in email are not opened from the Library.
- **No file editing** beyond the copy behaviour above.

## Storage

**Storage** (Library) shows what the business keeps: used and plan size, for example "4.2 GB of 25 GB in your plan". Past the
plan: "files cost $0.20 per GB a month on your invoice, never your credits". The sheet lists the biggest files and frees space
when you remove one.

## If something is wrong

| Symptom | Likely cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **No documents yet** | Nothing made or uploaded | **New**, a template, or **Upload** |
| A document is in the wrong topic | Its name does not say what it is | Rename it, or use **All** |
| **Nothing matches** | A filter or search is too narrow | **Show all** |
| Upload refuses a file | Over 1 GB, wrong type, or more than 10 at once | Use a smaller file or share a link; upload the rest after |
| A share link stopped working | Someone turned it off | Make a new link |
| Storage is over the plan | Large files are kept | Remove what you no longer need, or check the plan |
