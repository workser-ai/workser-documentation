**Business records are the facts of your business, kept where your AI agents can work on them: customers, orders, products,
invoices, campaigns, bookings and the rest.** They live in the **Data** tab, in 19 tables across five groups, plus any kinds
of record you add yourself.

Turn them on once in **Settings › Business → Use Workser business records** ("Customers, orders, bookings and stock."). Once
on, AI agents can read and update products, stock, customers and orders, and **Open the shop** appears.

## Open a table or one record

- **From the "+" launcher**: search a customer, order or product, or pick a table under its group.
- **From the Data tab**: the tables are grouped the way the business works.
- **From a link**: a customer on an invoice, an order on a payment, or a record from another record opens in its own tab.

A table is a list you can search, filter, sort and save views of. Some tables also offer **Board** (cards by status) or
**Calendar** (records on their date). A row opens the full record, with its fields, the records it is linked to, its history,
and on some records a **Map** card of links, files and notes that belong to it alone. Editing saves in place; required fields
are checked before it saves.

## The 19 tables

### Your business

| Table | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| **Customers** | Manage customers and their history |
| **Orders** | Follow every order from sale to delivery |
| **Payments** | Money received and given back — this tab opens your Stripe dashboard, not a Workser list |
| **Deliveries** | Packing and delivery status |
| **Products** | What you sell, with prices and stock |

### Appointments

| Table | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| **Bookings** | Appointments, times and deposits |
| **Staff** | Who works when, and what they offer |

### Selling

| Table | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| **Collections** | Group products to sell together |
| **Discounts** | Codes and offers for customers |
| **Carts** | Shoppers who haven't checked out yet |
| **Shipping** | Where you deliver and what it costs |

### Sales and support

| Table | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| **Deals** | Move sales opportunities toward a close |
| **Support** | Answer customer questions and issues |
| **Quotes** | Send proposals and win the work |
| **Invoices** | Bill customers and get paid |
| **Subscriptions** | Recurring plans and renewals |

### Marketing and website

| Table | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| **Campaigns** | Plan and send your marketing |
| **Pages** | The pages of your website |
| **Blog posts** | Articles that bring people to you |

**Subscriptions have no Add button**: they appear here when someone subscribes, and follow Stripe. **Payments** is the
project's Stripe dashboard. Invoices and quotes have their own guides:
[invoices, proposals and getting paid](/using/invoices-and-payments).

## What is not here

- **No CSV import or export** and **no bulk edit**. Records are added one at a time, or arrive from Stripe, Shopify, HubSpot or
  Salesforce sync.
- **No archive and no trash.** Deleting a record is permanent: "It is removed from your records for good, with its own fields
  and links." Cancelling an order or a booking is the reversible way to retire one.
- **No geographic map view.** The **Map** card belongs to one record; it is not a map of all records.

## Your own records

Some things your business tracks have no table in Workser: vehicles, properties, cases, equipment. Add a **kind of record**
in **Settings › Business → Your own records**, and it appears in Data with its own list and records. You can also show a
table from your project's database as records; the table itself is not changed.

## Your own fields

On any record kind, **Settings › Business → Your fields** adds typed fields:

| Field type | Use it for |
| --- | --- |
| **Text** | Names, references, notes |
| **Choice / status** | A fixed list, with your own labels |
| **Money** | An amount and its currency |
| **Record link** | Point at another record, like the customer on an order |
| **AI field** | A value an AI agent fills from other fields, with instructions and an output type |

Each AI fill uses credits. Fields can be renamed, hidden or deleted (with a confirmation); a hidden field does not show on the
record but its data stays.

## Who sees which records

Record access is separate from the business roles. **Settings › Business → Who sees what** maps each role to **Hidden**,
**See** or **Change** per record kind, can hide particular fields, and can limit locations. People and AI agents get one role
or none; **none means they see everything**. Owners and admins always see everything and are the only ones who change this.

An AI agent follows the same rule: if its role hides a record kind, it cannot read or change it, whatever you ask in chat.

## Keeping records in step with your apps

**Settings › Business → Connected systems** syncs HubSpot, Salesforce and Shopify: what crosses, which way, who wins per
field, the last run, rows set aside, **Retry** and **Sync now**. Core runs it every 10 minutes while on. It never creates
pipelines or stages, and orders only ever come in from Shopify.

## If something is missing

| Symptom | Likely cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Data is empty | Business records are off | **Settings › Business** → **Use Workser business records** |
| A table is missing | The business's pack does not include it, or it is your own kind and not added | **Kind of business** / **Your own records** |
| An AI agent cannot see a record | Its record role is **Hidden** | **Who sees what** |
| A field is read-only | Your role is **See**, or the field is hidden | Ask an owner or admin |
| A record will not save | A required field is empty | Fill the field the form marks |
