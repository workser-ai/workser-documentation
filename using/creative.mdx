**Creative is where your AI agents make visual work: posts and ads, presentations, video, audio, storyboards, whiteboards and
app screens.** Each design opens in its own tab with a canvas, and Workser Creative works in the chat beside it.

## Start one

**Creative** in the workspace lists your designs by kind, with a template row and search. **New** opens the picker:

| Kind | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| **Creative and ads** | Posts, ads, stories, flyers, banners |
| **Presentation** | Pitch, sales, company profile, reports |
| **Video** | Short videos and video ads |
| **Audio** | Voice-overs, sound effects, music, recordings turned into words |
| **Storyboard** | Plan a video ad scene by scene |
| **Whiteboard** | Brainstorms, flows, plans, meetings |
| **App design** | Screens for apps and websites |

Pick a template and it opens **instantly and free**, ready to edit. Or describe what you want ("A post for Friday's new
bread, in every size") and **Ask Workser Creative**; the design opens while it works.

## The studio

- The **canvas** fills the tab. Undo and redo are in the header (**⌘Z**, **⇧⌘Z**).
- **Ask Workser Creative** opens the design's own chat. It remembers what you said before, and every change appears on the
  canvas.
- **Comments** pin a note to something on the design. Name **@Workser Creative** and it makes the change.
- **History** lists versions with who made each; **Restore** puts an older one back (as a new version, so nothing is lost).
- **Full screen** hides the app around the design; **Esc** returns.

Edits save as you go. If someone else — or an AI agent — saves at the same time, Workser says so and never overwrites their
version: "Someone saved this design meanwhile (maybe Mia). Your last change wasn't saved." **Show their version** compares.

## Export

Export is made in your browser, full size and free:

- **PNG**, **JPG** or **PDF** for posts, screens and whiteboards.
- **PowerPoint** for presentations, with speaker notes.
- A multi-board design can export **this board** or **all boards**.

**Video** is the exception: an MP4 or MOV is rendered for you and charged by render time, only when the file is made. The
button becomes **Download MP4** when it is ready; you can leave the page, and it lands under **Files**.

## Special kinds

- **Storyboard** lays scenes on a canvas with words, voice, camera, length and a picture. **Make the clips** is refused until
  every scene has a real picture: "Scene 3 still shows the template's sample photo."
- **Audio** has **Read it aloud**, **Sound effect**, **Music**, **Change the voice**, **Remove noise** and **Get the words**.
  The price shows before you make it, and you are charged only when it works.
- **App design** also shows **From your apps, by Workser Code**: your app's screens on the same canvas, with **Generate a
  screen** and **Open in Workser Code**.
- **Presentation** has **Present** (⌘↵) and keeps speaker notes with the deck and in the .pptx.

## What is not built

- **Posting and scheduling** to social networks. The post-copy panel says it plainly: "Copy it and post it yourself for now.
  Posting and scheduling from Workser come next."
- **The Shorts and Cinema video studios** are marked **Soon**.
- **Approve bars** on storyboards; **Make the clips** is the action, in the More menu.

## If something is wrong

| Symptom | Likely cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Nothing made yet** | No design in this project | **New**, or ask in the chat beside the tab |
| **Creative is being set up** | The design service is updating | Try again after the server update |
| A change did not save | Someone else saved first | **Show their version**, then reapply your change |
| **Make the clips** is refused | A scene still uses the sample picture, or has none | Add a picture to every scene |
| A video export is missing | The render is still running, or it failed | Check **Files**; a failed render charges nothing |
| Text overflows in every size | The size set does not fit the words | Ask Workser Creative to "make every size" again |
