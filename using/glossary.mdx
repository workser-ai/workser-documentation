**Every Workser word, in the sense the app uses it.** Where a word has a page, the link goes deeper.

## Work

| Term | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **AI agent** | A role you hire (Customer Support, Bookkeeper, Designer…) with its own instructions, tools, memory and access. It is not a chat window: it keeps the role between Tasks. |
| **Workser** | The AI agent that leads your team on every plan. It takes a Task you send to "Workser" and hands it to the right AI agent, or does it itself. |
| **Task** | One piece of work you give in Chat, with steps, a result and its own credit cost. The app never calls it a "job". |
| **Chat** | The conversation a Task runs in. A chat keeps its own tabs, Task history and result. |
| **Automation** | Standing work an AI agent does without being asked: on a schedule, when an email arrives, on a connector event, before a meeting, or by keeping an eye out. |
| **Automation schedule** | How often an Automation may start (Starter: every hour; Pro: every 15 minutes). Not a separate product word. |
| **Stuck** | A Task that cannot continue. It says what it needs, and the ask waits in Requests. |
| **Requests** | The one list of everything waiting for you: questions, approvals and Stuck Tasks. The page is titled Requests; "Needs you" is the state a Task or AI agent is in. |
| **Ask** | The card in Chat that asks you something: a question, a permission, an approval or a "this stopped". |
| **Approval** | A risky step waiting for your yes: refunds, deletes, cancellations, payments from $50, a new contact, or a card purchase. |
| **Passkey** | The confirmation your device makes when you approve money. The passkey page belongs to the card service, never to the chat. |

## Your AI agent

| Term | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Profile** | The panel tab that says how one AI agent works: instructions, skills, connectors, memory and when it checks with you. |
| **Autonomy** | How far an AI agent goes on its own: asks first, sends and tells you, or auto-approves a kind of action. |
| **Probation** | The first Tasks after hiring, when a new AI agent checks more with you. |
| **Skill** | A step-by-step way of doing something, written in your words, that your AI agents can use. |
| **Agent kit** | Everything that makes an AI agent good at its job, saved as one set so you can set up another like it. |
| **MCP server** | A source of extra tools an AI agent may use, connected through the Model Context Protocol. |
| **Memory** | What an AI agent knows: facts, a map of the business, Task notes and anything waiting for your check. |
| **Company knowledge** | The facts, products, customers and tone every AI agent follows, plus your Never rules. |
| **Never rules** | Hard rules checked before an action. If a rule says never, the AI agent does not do it. |
| **Your rules** | The shared rules in Settings › Your rules that every AI agent follows. |

## Where the work happens

| Term | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Cloud computer** | The computer a project's AI agents work on, in the cloud. It keeps working when your laptop is closed. |
| **Sandbox** | A private working copy for one Task. A Task that runs in its own sandbox is cleaned up when it ends. |
| **Logins** | Saved credentials for your apps. An AI agent fills them in by name and never sees the password. |
| **Connector** | An app or service your AI agents may use (Gmail, Slack, Shopify, Stripe and more), connected once and granted per AI agent. |
| **Front desk** | One business address and number behind the whole team: who wrote, who answered, and the work behind it. |
| **Procedure** | How your business does a recurring thing, written down so the work is done the same way every time. |
| **Workspace tab** | A thing opened beside Chat: a computer, a browser, a file, a record, an app, a result, a document. |
| **Result** | Everything one Task made: documents, sheets, slides, images, pages, files. |
| **File** | One uploaded or made file. **Documents** are the Library's files, with folders, share links and due dates. |
| **Record** | One row of business data: a customer, an order, an invoice, a booking. |
| **Project** | One workspace with its own computer, files, apps, card and people. A business can hold several. |
| **Business** | Your organization on Workser: its people, billing, plan and projects. |

## Money

| Term | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Credit** | What work costs, charged as it runs. 100 credits = $1 when you pay. |
| **Allowance** | The monthly USD limit you give one AI agent for card purchases. |
| **Task spending limit** | The pause on a single paid Task after it uses 10% of the month's credits, so one Task cannot quietly use the month. |
| **Money-back guarantee** | Within 7 days of your first plan payment: your plan price back, minus the credits you used. |
