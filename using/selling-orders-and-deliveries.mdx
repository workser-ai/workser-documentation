**Orders in Workser follow a sale from the first line item to the delivery at the door.** They live in **Data › Orders**,
beside **Customers**, **Products**, **Deliveries**, **Payments** and **Shipping**. An AI agent can create an order, chase it
and start a delivery; a refund is for a person.

## The order list

The list is a table, not a board. Its first columns are **Customer**, **Placed**, **Status**, **Payment** and **Total**, then
any other visible field. Search, filter, sort and saved views work as in every Data table.

A **Which orders** filter separates real money from tests:

- **Real orders**
- **Paid in live mode**
- **Test orders**
- **All orders**

Orders and Customers carry a **Needs attention** line (for example "3 unpaid orders"), and it opens exactly the records it
counts. Empty: "No orders yet. Add one, or ask an AI agent to." A filtered empty says "No orders match those filters."

## Take an order

**Add order** opens **New order**:

| Field | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| **Order number** | Required, for example `INV-1042` |
| **Type** | Sale, Booking, Subscription or Invoice |
| **Status** | Draft, Pending, Paid or Completed (cancelling is a separate action) |
| **Currency** | Prefilled from the business |
| **Customer (optional)** | Search existing customers, or leave it blank |
| **Line items** | Name, price and quantity per row, with a product picker: **Search products…**, **Create new product** |

Price is typed as you say it (12.50); Workser stores cents. **Create order** confirms "Order created."

## Move an order along

The **Manage** section offers the one move that fits the current state:

| From | Button | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Unpaid / open | **Mark as paid** | For money taken outside Workser, like cash or a bank transfer. The order is then paid in full. |
| Paid | **Close** | Paid and finished, so it leaves the open list |
| Completed | **Open again** | Puts the closed order back in the open list |

A cancelled or refunded order offers no move.

## Cancel an order

**Cancel order** asks first: **"Cancel order #1042?"** — "The order is marked cancelled, its stock goes back and its open
deliveries stop. A payment already taken isn't refunded here."

Choose a reason: **The customer changed their mind**, **Looked like fraud**, **Not enough stock**, **The payment was
declined**, **Another reason**. Then **Cancel the order**. A cancelled order cannot be reopened or refunded from this screen.

## Refund an order

**Refund** is for an owner or admin, and only when money was taken and some is left. The sheet says
**"Refund order #1042?"**, how much is left, and "The money goes back to the customer's card. This can't be undone."

- **Amount (USD)**, **Why** (The customer asked, Wrong or damaged item, Charged by mistake, The order was cancelled,
  Something else) and an optional **Note**.
- **Keep it** or **Refund**. Success: "Refunded $48.00."
- Errors: "Refund an amount more than zero." / "You can refund up to $48.00."

A part-refund leaves the rest refundable.

## Deliveries

The **Delivery** section lists each delivery as a **Shipment · Pending**-style row. Empty: "Nothing sent, picked up or
booked for this order yet."

**Start a delivery** asks for a **Kind of delivery** (Shipment, Digital, Appointment, Service visit, Pickup), a
**Carrier (optional)** and a **Tracking number (optional)**, then **Start it**.

- Open deliveries offer **Mark done**, **Hold**, and **Release** when on hold.
- A delivery has its own record: Order, Type, Status, Carrier, Tracking, Shipped, Expected, Delivered, Scheduled, Where,
  With, Ready, Picked up, Done, Cancelled.
- When it has not shipped, a **Location** section offers **Move to** and **Move** ("Moved to Downtown.").
- Tracking is a link when it is an `https` address.

Shipping addresses, methods and amounts are fields on the order and the delivery; zones and rates live in the **Shipping**
table.

## What changes stock

Cancelling says the stock goes back, and that is core's behaviour. The web app itself never edits stock on cancel, refund or
delivery; stock changes on the **Shop** page and on the product record. The product record says: "Change stock and prices on
the Shop page; your AI agents read the same list."

## If something is wrong

| Symptom | Likely cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| An order is missing from the list | The **Which orders** filter hides test or live orders | Switch to **All orders** |
| No **Refund** button | The payment was not taken, it is fully refunded, or you are not an owner or admin | Check **Payment**; ask an owner or admin |
| **Mark as paid** is gone | The order is already paid or completed | Use **Close** or **Open again** |
| A delivery cannot move | It has shipped, or there is no other location | Move it before it ships |
| A cancelled order needs money back | Cancelling does not refund | Refund before cancelling, or use the payment's refund in Stripe |
