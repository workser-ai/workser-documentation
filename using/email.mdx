**A work email gives one AI agent its own address, so customers and suppliers write to a person, not to you.** It is an
add-on on a paid plan: **$2 a month per inbox**, on your plan's invoice. You read everything it sent and got in the AI agent's
**Email** tab, and an email to someone new waits for your yes.

## Before you start

- **Plan:** Starter, Pro or Team. **Pro includes one work email**; everywhere else it is an add-on. No plan includes a free
  inbox, and a business without a plan cannot add one.
- **Who can do it:** an owner or admin gives or deletes an address. A member can read the mail.
- **What is included:** 200 emails a month per inbox, sent and received together. After that, **0.2 credits per email** in
  either direction. Emails that bounce or are blocked do not count.
- **Where it lives:** Settings › Identity › Email, and the AI agent's **Email** tab in the workspace.

## Give an AI agent an address

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings › Identity">
    The page has four tabs: **AI agents**, **Email**, **Phone** and **Logins**. Open **Email**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the AI agent">
    Under **Work email**, choose the AI agent that should have the address.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Give the inbox">
    On Pro, the included inbox is claimed with **Claim it**, with no new charge. Otherwise **Give an email · $2 a month**
    asks you to confirm **Add for $2 a month** first. Up to 50 inboxes can be bought.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send a test">
    Write to the new address. It appears in the AI agent's **Inbox**, and the AI agent can answer it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Deleting an address removes it from the AI agent: mail to it bounces, and it can't be restored. **Past mail and what each
Task did with it stay.** The $2 a month continues until you lower the number of inboxes, so a new address costs nothing extra.
If sending is paused ("Sending is paused: too many bounces"), **Resume sending** turns it back on.

## The Email tab

| Folder | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| **Inbox** | Mail sent to the address, with what the AI agent did about it. Empty: "No mail received yet". |
| **Sent** | What the AI agent sent. Empty: "Nothing sent yet". |
| **Outbox** | Emails waiting for your OK, on their way, or not sent. Empty: "Nothing waiting to send". |

At the top of the **Outbox**, **Waiting for you** lists every email that needs a decision, marked **Needs your OK**. Each
one shows who it goes to and its subject, and opens the draft:

- **Approve and send** (or **Approve and forward**).
- **Ask \<agent\> to change it**, which puts a change request in the chat.
- **Discard**, after a confirmation.

Answering says **"Approved. \<agent\> will send it."** or **"Declined. \<agent\> won't send it."** If someone else answered
it first, the tab says "This was already answered, maybe in the chat."

Open a conversation to read each message in order. There is no compose box in the Email tab: writing is the AI agent's
work. **Ask \<agent\> to reply**, **Ask \<agent\> to forward it** or **Ask \<agent\> to read it to you** fills the chat
message box (for example "Reply to Ana about “Order 1042”: "), and you send it as a Task.

Use **Search this mailbox…** to find mail by sender, subject or first line. Attachments are shown as tiles with their name
and size, not opened from here.

## What waits for your yes

- **An email to someone you have not trusted yet.** After you trust a contact, its mail can go without asking. Trust is
  managed in **Logins**.
- **An email the AI agent is unsure about.** It asks rather than guesses.

Everything else, the AI agent sends on its own according to its [Autonomy](/using/autonomy-and-probation) rules.

## One inbox or all of them

- **The workspace Email tab** shows one AI agent's mailbox, or every inbox when no AI agent is chosen.
- **An AI agent's Email view** (in its panel) is the same mail for that AI agent.
- **An Automation can start when an email arrives**, and answer from your shipping policy, for example. See
  [Automations](/ai-workforce/automations).

## What it costs

- **$2 a month per inbox**, on the plan's invoice. Pro includes one.
- **200 emails a month included**; then 0.2 credits per email, in either direction.
- Reading and searching mail does not cost credits; the AI agent's work on it does, as any Task.

## If something is wrong

| Symptom | Likely cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| An email the customer sent never arrives | Wrong address, or the sender bounced | Check the address on the AI agent's Identity tab; send a test |
| The AI agent cannot send | Sending is paused, or the email waits for your OK | **Resume** sending, or answer it in the Outbox |
| "This was already answered" | Someone answered it in the chat | Nothing; the Outbox refreshes |
| The inbox is over its 200 | More than 200 sent and received this month | Each extra email is 0.2 credits; nothing is refused |
| No address can be added | The plan is below Starter, or the business has no plan | Upgrade, or ask an owner or admin |
