**Settings is one sheet over whatever you are doing: open it from the left sidebar and every row changes the sheet, not
the page.** There are 18 rows in four groups (plus two that open by link), with search at the top. With no row chosen, **Credits and usage** opens. On a
phone the rows are a list and a row opens its own page with a back arrow.

Search matches a row's label and its one-line hint. A search with no match says "Nothing matches “\<what you typed\>”."

Deep links open a row directly: `?sheet=settings:plan`, `settings:card`, `settings:skills`, and so on. Old names still
work: `money`, `wallet` and `cards` open **Card and spending**; `phone` and `email` open **Identity**; `chat-apps` opens
**Chat apps**; `business` opens **Business**; `budget` and `report` open **Linked projects**.

## Plan and credits

| Row | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Credits and usage** | Every charge with its units and price, and what is left. The place to check what a Task or an AI agent cost. Owners and admins only. |
| **Plan and billing** | Your plan, invoices, payment method, extra projects, the 7-day money-back guarantee and your add-ons. Owners and admins only. |

## Your team

| Row | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **AI workforce** | Every AI agent in the project, one row per agent. A row opens its Manage dialog, where its model, identity and rules live. |
| **How AI agents work** | Probation for a new AI agent, and whether to keep how long it actually worked on each Task. |
| **Your rules** | The shared rules every AI agent in the business follows. |
| **Skills** | The step-by-step abilities your AI agents can use, written in your words. |
| **Computers** | Where your AI agents work: the shared computer every AI agent uses, and any dedicated add-on computers. Watch, reset, rename or remove one here. |
| **Computer updates** (link only) | New apps and fixes for a computer: automatic, ask me, or off. Not on the rail for this launch; open it by link. |

## Apps and accounts

| Row | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Chat apps** | Where people message your AI agents: WhatsApp, Telegram, Slack, Discord and LINE, with the setup checklist for each. |
| **Connectors** | The software your AI agents can work in, and MCP servers. Connected once for the project, granted per AI agent. |
| **Identity** | Each AI agent's work email, phone number and logins, in four tabs: AI agents, Email, Phone, Logins. |
| **Business** | Your business records, your own fields, sync, who can see what, the booking page and payouts. |
| **Card and spending** | The project card, every order an AI agent asked to place, each AI agent's limit and stores, and single-use cards. Starter and up. |

## You and your business

| Row | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **People** | Who can open the business, their role, inviting someone, changing a role and removing someone. |
| **General** | The project's picture, name, description and details. Deleting the project lives here too. |
| **Linked projects** | Projects this one manages, each marked Client or Internal. |
| **Your account** | Your name, picture, password and sign-in. |
| **Notifications** | Texts when something needs you (text alerts are not live yet) and notifications on this device. |
| **Appearance** | Light, Dark, or follow your device. |

## Rows that are not on the rail

- **Computer updates** opens by link only for this launch (`?sheet=settings:computer-updates`).
- **Your computer** needs the paused Mac app, so it stays off the rail; it opens by link (`?sheet=settings:my-computer`).

At the foot of the rail, **Guides and help** opens these docs in a new tab.

## Who can change what

- **Owner and admin:** plan and billing, connecting the card, approving purchases, people and invitations, business
  settings, computers and chat apps, and deleting the project.
- **Member:** works in the same projects with the same AI agents and fills in business records; cannot change plan, billing,
  people, business settings, computers or chat apps. The row says so when you try.
- **View only:** a label chosen when someone is invited, meant for reading. The app's own checks are strongest on owner and
  admin actions; record-level access is set separately in **Business › Who sees what**.

Owners and admins can change a member's role from their row in **People**. You cannot change your own role or the owner's;
ownership transfers are handled by Workser.
