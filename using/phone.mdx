**A work phone gives one AI agent its own number, so it can make and take calls and receive texts.** Sending texts comes later. It is an add-on on a paid
plan: **$5 a month per number** in the US and Canada, then calls and texts at cost + 30% in credits.

## Before you start

- **Plan:** Starter, Pro or Team. **Pro includes one phone number**; everywhere else it is an add-on.
- **Countries:** US and Canada numbers today. Numbers anywhere else are **Soon**.
- **Who can do it:** an owner or admin adds or gives back a number.
- **Where it lives:** Settings › Identity › Phone, and the AI agent's **Phone** tab.

## Give an AI agent a number

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings › Identity">
    The page has four tabs: **AI agents**, **Email**, **Phone** and **Logins**. Open **Phone**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the AI agent">
    Choose the AI agent the number belongs to. Each number belongs to one AI agent.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open a number">
    On Pro, the included number is claimed without a new charge. Otherwise **Open a number · $5 a month** asks you to confirm
    **Confirm · $5 a month** first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Call it">
    The number answers with the AI agent's voice. Its **Phone** tab shows the call as soon as it ends.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To end it, choose **Give it back** and confirm. Calls already kept stay in the project's activity.

## The Phone tab

The tab opens on the AI agent's number and its state:

| State | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Answering** | It takes calls now. |
| **Ready** | The number is set up and answering is on. |
| **Paused** | No active plan, so no calls or texts. Choose a plan to turn it back on; the number is released if you do not. |
| **Turned away** | **Stop answering** is on. **Answer calls again** turns it back on. |

The number's abilities are **Makes calls**, **Takes calls** and **Receives texts** ("Calls · Receives texts"). Below them,
**This month** shows **Calls**, **Talk time** and **Cost**, and the list holds each call with its summary, transcript and
cost:

- **Search** by person, number or what was said.
- **Open a call** for its summary, the conversation turn by turn, the recording, and the Tasks on either side.
- Empty: "No calls yet. Each call shows here with a summary, transcript and cost."

The workspace **Phone** tab shows one AI agent's calls, or all calls when no AI agent is chosen. An AI agent's own **Phone**
view is the same list for that AI agent.

## How it answers

**Call settings** (summarised in the **How it answers** card) opens the voice settings for that number:

| Setting | What it controls |
| --- | --- |
| **Language** | The language callers speak and the AI agent answers in |
| **Greeting** | The first thing it says; empty uses the standard greeting |
| **Call instructions** | What to ask, what to promise, who to pass to, in your own words |
| **Transfer to** | The US or Canada number that gets the call when someone asks for a person; empty keeps every call |
| **Voicemail message** | What it leaves when it calls someone and reaches voicemail |
| **Hang up after silence** | How many seconds of silence end the call |
| **More voice options** | Voice, speaking speed, listening carefully for names and numbers, when a caller cuts in, and **Record calls** |
| **Only answer during business hours** | The days, times and time zone it answers; **When closed, say** is the message outside them |
| **Do not contact** | Numbers it never calls or texts, including anyone who texted STOP |

Callers are told by law that they are speaking with an AI agent, and that the call may be recorded when **Record calls** is
on. Workser adds that line to the greeting for you.

**What it knows on calls** is a separate set of short notes — opening hours, what to say about prices, where to park. Add a
title and the words to use; the next call can use it.

## What it costs

- **$5 a month per number**, on the plan's invoice. Pro includes one.
- **Calls and texts** are charged in credits at the provider's cost + 30%, as they happen.
- The AI agent's work during a call is charged like any other Task.

## If something is wrong

| Symptom | Likely cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Calls do not connect | The number is paused, or **Stop answering** is on | Check the state at the top of the Phone tab; **Answer calls again** |
| It answers but says the wrong thing | The greeting or call instructions are out of date | **How it answers** → edit, then **Save voice** |
| A transfer fails | **Transfer to** is empty or is not a US or Canada number | Set a US or Canada number |
| No transcript appears | The call has not finished, or recording is off | Wait for the call to end; check the recording setting |
| No number can be added | The plan is below Starter, or the country is not supported | Upgrade, or use email and chat apps |
| The number is **Paused** | The plan is not active | Choose a plan; the number is released if it stays unpaid |
| The number is gone | Someone chose **Give it back** | Add a new number; old calls stay in activity |
