**Everyone in your business works in the same projects, with the same AI agents, records and Company knowledge.** Roles decide
who can change the business and who can only work in it. **Settings › People** is where you invite people, change a role and
remove someone.

## Invite someone

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings › People">
    Owners and admins see the member list, any pending invitations, and **Invite someone**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Type their email">
    Under **Invite someone**, enter the address they will sign in with.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose what they can do">
    **Can**: **Member**, **Admin** or **View only**. The default is Member.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Press Invite">
    The note confirms **"Invitation sent to …"** with the address. Until they accept, they show as **Invited** with **Cancel**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The invitation is a link they open and sign in with. If they already have a Workser account, they join at once; if not, they
join when they sign up with that address.

**Only owners and admins can invite or remove people.** Everyone else sees that line instead of the form.

## The four roles

| | Owner | Admin | Member | View only |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Invite and remove people | Yes | Yes | No | No |
| Change someone's role | Yes | Yes | No | No |
| Change the plan, add credits, open invoices | Yes | Yes | No | No |
| Connect the card, approve a purchase | Yes | Yes | No | No |
| Change business settings, records switch, fields, who sees what | Yes | Yes | No | No |
| Add or change computers and chat apps | Yes | Yes | No | No |
| Rename or delete the project | Yes | Yes | No | No |
| Work in projects, give Tasks, fill records | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Connect a connector | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Disconnect a connector shared by every project | Yes | Yes | No | No |

**Owner** is the person who created the business, and there is one. **Admin** can do everything the owner can except change
the owner. **Member** works in the business but cannot change how it runs. **View only** is chosen for someone who should read
rather than change the work.

**View only is a reading role, not a hard wall in every screen.** The business settings, billing and people are owner/admin
only, and record access follows **Settings › Business → Who sees what**; the rest of the app's own checks treat View only like
a member. Give it to people you trust to read only.

## Change someone's role

Each member row has their role (Member, Admin or View only). Pick another and it saves at once; the note confirms the new
role. You cannot change your own role or the owner's. Ownership transfers are handled by Workser.

## Remove someone

**Remove** next to the person asks first: "They can't open this business any more. Invite them again to bring them back."
They lose access at once; their past work stays.

## Seats and the Team plan

Starter and Pro are priced for one person. **Team** is **$20 per person a month**, two people minimum, with 2,200 credits per
person shared by everyone. Stripe asks how many people when you buy Team; this web app has no separate seat counter.

## What people share

Everyone in the business sees the same projects, AI agents, Automations, Logins and Company knowledge. Nothing has to be set
up twice. What each person can **change** is the role; what each can **see in records** is the record role; what an AI agent
can use is set per AI agent.

## If something is wrong

| Symptom | Likely cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The invite never arrived | Wrong address, or spam | Cancel it and invite again |
| "That person is already in this business." | The email is already a member | Change their role instead |
| "Only owners and admins can do that." | Your role | Ask an owner or admin |
| A person cannot see credits | Credits and usage is owner/admin only | Ask an owner or admin |
| A person cannot edit a record | Their record role is **See** or **Hidden** | **Business → Who sees what** |
| "The owner's role can't be changed here." | The row is the owner | Transfer ownership through Workser |
