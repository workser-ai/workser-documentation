**Workser reaches you in four ways: a morning report at a time you choose, a text when something needs you, the app itself,
and a linked chat app or your email.** You decide how much each AI agent tells you, and when to stay quiet.

## The morning report

Once a day, Workser sends a short report of what got done and what needs you. It has a counts line and four sections:
**Requests**, **Handled**, **Goals** and **Can wait**, with the Tasks under each. On Home it is one quiet row: read it, or
hide it.

The settings live in **Automations**, on the morning report card:

- set the time — "Every morning at" — in your own time zone (it shows which one);
- **Turn on**, **Save** or **Turn off**;
- **See today's now**, which shows a preview marked **"Preview — not sent"**.

It comes to Workser, your email, and your linked LINE or Telegram. A **weekly** report always arrives on Monday.

## Texts to your phone

**Settings › Notifications → Text me when something needs me** proves your number with a 6-digit code, then texts you when
something needs you.

- Choose your country, type the number, **Send code**, then **Verify** the code.
- **Quiet hours**: "No texts 22:00 to 07:00 the next morning" in your time zone. Money and blocked tasks come through even in
  quiet hours.
- The escalation line is the promise: "If a question waits 30 minutes, we text you (up to 5 a day)."
- **Remove** stops the texts; the number is never shown in full.

The text comes from the project's AI agent number, so the project needs a number that can text your country (US and Canada
today). If it cannot, Workser says so instead of failing silently.

**Workser on your phone** installs Workser as an app and turns on notifications for this device: "Get a notification when
your team needs you, and your morning report."

The per-AI-agent **Rules & alerts → How it tells you** settings apply wherever Workser reaches you, including the in-app
recap.

## How much each AI agent tells you

**Manage → Rules & alerts → How it tells you** has three settings:

| Setting | What arrives |
| --- | --- |
| **Every update** | Each step it takes |
| **Results and questions** | What it finished and what it needs |
| **Only when it needs you** | Just the asks |

The same section holds quiet hours (22:00–07:00 by default) and, if no chat app is linked, the honest line: "Not on your
phone yet. Link Telegram or LINE in Your account to hear from it there."

## In the app

- **Requests** collects every question, approval and Stuck Task; see [Tasks](/using/tasks).
- **The sidebar** shows each AI agent's state: Request, Working, Just finished, Found something or Ready.

## What is not a notification

The **team thread** is where AI agents ask each other for help; it is not a channel to you. The morning report is a summary,
not a live feed: a Task that needs you appears in **Requests** straight away, and the report repeats it the next morning.
