**Chat is where you give work in plain words.** Type in the box at the bottom ("Give your team a task, or ask anything"), and
Workser picks the right AI agent — or you pick one yourself, and the box says "Message \<name\>". A Task starts when you press
Send, and the chat shows what the AI agent is doing until it is Done.

## Send a Task

<Steps>
  <Step title="Say what you want, as you would to a new hire">
    "Match last month's payments to our invoices", "Draft replies to this week's reviews for my OK", "Turn this sheet into a
    short report".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose who takes it (optional)">
    Leave it on Workser and the hint applies: **"The right AI agent picks it up"**. Or name one AI agent and the box becomes
    **Message \<name\>**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add what it needs">
    Attach up to 10 files, or type **@** to add a tab, app, AI agent, computer, file, connector, record or earlier chat. The
    model chip above the box picks the model. See [AI models](/using/models).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send">
    The Task is charged in credits as it runs. A start screen shows an estimate when enough similar Tasks have finished, and
    the Task's own sheet shows the credits used so far.
  </Step>
</Steps>

While it runs, the send button becomes **Stop** when the box is empty. It asks "Stop this task? What it has done so far is
kept." — **Stop task** or **Keep going**. Work already done stays.

## While it runs

- **A live line** under the AI agent's name says **Working**, **Starting · …** or **Request · …** with the Task's title.
- **Every step** appears in the chat as it happens, with what it used and, when a step fails, what it said.
- **The AI agent's screen** is one click away: open its panel → **Today**, or the Task's **Watch** view.
- **It asks when it is risky.** The ask is docked above the message box; the Task waits. See [Tasks](/using/tasks).
- **Credits used so far** are on the Task's own sheet (the credit meter), not in the chat header.

## One Task, one chat, many chats

The chats list keeps every conversation. **Search chats**, or use the jump strip at the top of a chat to go back to an
earlier one. A chat remembers the workspace tabs you opened in it, so returning to it returns to that work.

Tasks at once by plan: Starter 5, Pro 10, Team 5 per person. When the plan or the computer is busy, the Task says **Waiting
for a computer** or **Starting soon (2 in line)** and begins on its own; it is queued, not refused.

## When it ends

A finished Task is checked against what you asked before it says **Done**. You see:

- **Its answer** in the chat.
- **What it made**: a result card (documents, sheets, slides, images, pages), each opening in its own tab.
- **What it changed**, if it changed an app: a **Changes** tab with a diff and Preview, Publish or Undo.
- **What it wants next**, when the AI agent suggests a follow-up Task. You start it or dismiss it.

If it cannot finish, it says **Stuck** and what it needs. The ask waits in [Requests](/using/tasks).

## What a chat is not

- It is not a general chatbot: the AI agent has your business's rules, logins and files, and it works in your apps.
- It is not a queue of prompts: **Send** starts a Task with a start, steps, a result and a cost.
- It is not private from your team: people in the business see the same projects and Tasks, according to their role. See
  [Settings](/using/settings) for who can change what.
