**Marketing in Workser is three tables in the Data tab: Campaigns, Discounts, and the Pages and Blog posts of your website.**
An AI agent can write a campaign, plan the audience and schedule it; sending is still your call.

## Campaigns

**Data › Campaigns** lists every campaign with its status. An email campaign opens in the **Composer** screen, not a form.

### Make one

**New campaign** asks for:

- **Name**
- **Type**: **Email** or **SMS**
- **Subject** (email)
- **Description**
- **Message**
- **AI generated**: "Mark this campaign as AI-authored."

**Create** says "Draft campaign made." It starts as a draft; scheduling and sending happen on its page.

### The campaign page

| Section | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Results** | One headline ("Sent to 1,240 · 38% opened · 12 sales, ฿18,400") and **The numbers** |
| **Message** | The subject and the message as it will go out |
| **Audience** | "80 people will get this", and who is left out and why |
| **Sending** | Email only: progress, the time, the sender, **Send from** when there are several inboxes |
| **Offer** | The discount this campaign carries, if any |
| **Daily results** | Social and ads campaigns, or days already recorded |

Statuses are **Draft**, **Scheduled**, **Sending**, **Sent**, **Paused** and **Ended**.

### The actions

| State | Buttons |
| --- | --- |
| Draft with people to send to | **Send to 80 people** (or "Send to 1 person") and **Schedule…** |
| Draft with nobody to send to | **Schedule…** only |
| Scheduled / sending | **Pause**, **Send now** (or "Send the other 40 now"), **End** |
| Paused | **Resume**, **End** |
| Sent | **End** |

**Schedule…** asks for **Send on**, then **Schedule**; the note says it goes out from the team address at that time.
**Send now** asks first: "Send “Spring Sale” to 80 people? The emails go out from team@… right away and can't be taken back."
Over 500 in a day, the rest follow the next day. **End** is final: "It stops for good and can't be started again. Its
results stay here."

### Audience and consent

There are no hand-made segments. The audience is the people the campaign's channel can reach, and the screen is honest about
it: "12 in this audience haven't agreed to email, so they won't get it. Anyone who unsubscribes is left out."

Consent is per customer, on the customer record: **Subscribe**, **Unsubscribe** or **Subscribe again**, with a confirmation
after an unsubscribe.

### Email vs SMS

- **Email** gets a subject, a sender, send-now and progress.
- **SMS** has the message and scheduling in this screen; there is no send-now or progress card here.

Opens and clicks only appear when they are above zero; core-sent email does not record opens, so do not expect an open rate
on every campaign.

### Senders

**Sending** names the sender: "Sent from team@…, an AI agent's email address." If no AI agent has an inbox, it says so and
asks you to give one an address first — see [Work email](/using/email).

## Discounts

**Data › Discounts** lists offers with their state: **Off**, **Ended**, **Scheduled**, **Used up** or **Active**.

**New discount** asks for a **Code**, **Title**, **Type** (**Percentage**, **Fixed amount**, **Free shipping**, **Buy X get Y**),
the amount or value, a currency where needed, a minimum purchase, a usage limit and whether it is active. The row menu has
**Edit**, **Sync to Stripe** and **Delete**.

A discount's own screen lists its codes:

- **Main code** (cannot be removed) and codes for one customer.
- **Make codes** in a batch: **How many (up to 100)**, **Starts with**, **Uses for each** ("No limit"), then **Make codes**.
- **Add a code**: Code, Uses allowed, Only for one customer, **Find a customer**, **Add code**.
- Each row says "3 of 10 used", "3 used" or "Not used yet". Removing asks: "SPRING3 stops working. Orders that already used it
  keep their discount."

## Pages and blog posts

**Pages** and **Blog posts** are ordinary records in the same group:

- A **Page** shows Status (draft, published, archived), Web address, Kind, Published, Written by, Updated and **Title in
  search results**.
- A **Blog post** adds Category, Summary and **Description in search results**.

They are website content, edited as records. The public **booking page** is separate: it is set up in **Settings › Business →
Booking page**, not built from a Page record.

## If something is wrong

| Symptom | Likely cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Send now** is missing | The campaign is SMS, or no AI agent has a work email | Schedule it, or give an AI agent an inbox |
| "No AI agent has an email address to send from yet" | No work email is set up | [Work email](/using/email) |
| Only some people will get it | They have not agreed to that channel | Check consent on the customer record |
| A code does not work | It is expired, off, used up, or limited to one customer | Check its state and codes |
| Opens show nothing | Core-sent email does not record opens | Judge it by sends, clicks and sales |
| A page link is wrong | The Web address field was changed | Edit the record's Web address |
