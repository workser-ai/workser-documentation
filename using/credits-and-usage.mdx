**Credits are what work costs in Workser, charged as it runs.** 100 credits = $1 when you pay. **Settings › Credits and usage**
shows every charge, with its units and price, and what is left. Only owners and admins can open it; everyone else sees "Only
owners and admins can see credits and usage. Ask an admin for access."

## Open the page

**Settings › Credits and usage** is the first row in Settings, and the default row when Settings opens with nothing chosen.

- **Pick a period:** 7, 30 or 90 days.
- **Read the chart:** charges grouped by category, each with its share.
- **Switch view:** **By task** (the default), **By AI agent** or **Every charge**.
- **Find one charge:** search by Task, AI agent or charge, or filter with **All AI agents**.
- **Recent top-ups:** credits you bought, newest first, each with its status: **Added**, **On its way**, **Refunded** or
  **Didn't go through**.

The balance is split in two: **Plan credits** ("1,200 of 2,000", used first, and what is left expires when the plan renews)
and **Bought credits** ("Never expire. Used after your plan credits."). A negative balance reads "−1,137 credits" with how
many more are needed to start work.

## What is charged

| What | When it is charged |
| --- | --- |
| **AI model calls** | The moment each call finishes, in the units the provider returns: input and output tokens |
| **Cloud computer and sandbox minutes** | When each piece finishes or fails, billed per second |
| **Phone** | Calls and texts when each piece finishes or fails, at the provider's cost + 30% |
| **Work email** | 200 emails a month per inbox are included; after that, 0.2 credits per email in either direction |
| **Cloud browser** | Not sold today (postponed) |
| **A card purchase** | The purchase goes on your card, not your credits. Workser's per-request fee for the card service is 0 today |

**A Task that fails pays for what actually ran, no more**, and its credits are not given back. That is why a failed Task can
still show a charge.

## Before and during a Task

- **An estimate** appears on a start screen once enough similar Tasks have finished: "Estimate: 12–30 credits, from what this
  AI agent's last N jobs like this were charged."
- **The live meter** is on the Task's own sheet: "14 credits so far", then "Charged 20 credits".
- **A single paid Task pauses and asks** after it uses **10% of the month's credits** — Starter 200, Pro 1,050, Team 10% of
  the team's shared credits (440 for two people). The pause is an ordinary approval card; with no answer in 4 hours the Task
  stops.
- **A Task with its own Credit limit per task** (Manage → Advanced) stops at that limit without asking.
- **Under 10 credits, nothing new starts.** You see **"You're out of credits"** and **"Your AI agents have stopped."**, with
  **Add credits** and **Upgrade plan**. A Task already running stops.

Nothing is billed past your credits: work stops until you add credits or change plan. The balance chip reads **Low** at 50
credits or fewer, and Workser emails you at 50%, 80% and 100% of the month's credits, then again when work stops.

For accounting, the organization's Credits page can download every charge as a CSV or a PDF.

## Add credits

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Add credits">
    From **Settings › Credits and usage**, the out-of-credits banner, or **Settings › Plan and billing**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose an amount">
    Any whole-dollar amount from **$3 to $1,000**, or a quick amount: $3, $5, $10, $25, $50 or $100.
  </Step>
  <Step title="See what you get">
    **$1 per 100 credits.** The sheet shows **You pay**, **You get** and **Balance after**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pay">
    One payment, no plan change, by card, Apple Pay or Google Pay. Credits you add never expire, and the page shows them
    under **Recent top-ups** once they land.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Only owners and admins can buy credits; a member sees "Ask an owner or admin to add credits."

If your balance is negative because work ran past the credits, the sheet says so and asks for at least the amount that covers
the shortfall before work can start again.

## Where the numbers come from

Plan credits and limits come from `constants/pricing.ts`; the rate is 100 credits per dollar; top-ups are whole-dollar units
of $1. The live plan values are at [workser.ai/pricing](https://workser.ai/pricing).
