**Workser is one web app at [workser.ai](https://workser.ai): Chat on the left of a project, workspace tabs beside it, and a
Settings sheet over the top.** This tab explains where to click, what happens next and what each screen is for. It is the
web app; [Workser Code](/code/overview) covers the desktop coding workspace.

If you are new, read [What is Workser?](/ai-workforce/overview) first, then [Give your first task](/ai-workforce/quickstart).
Come back here when you want to do something specific.

## Moving around

- **The "+" button** beside the tabs opens the launcher: search chats, calls, mail, purchases, files and apps, or start a
  new creative, app, Automation or upload.
- **A tab keeps its state** while you switch to another and back, and a chat remembers the tabs you opened in it.
- **Old links still work.** `?open=` opens a tab, `?sheet=settings:<row>` opens a Settings row, and old row names
  (for example `money`) open the row they became.

## What you need before you start

- **A plan.** Plans start at $20 a month: Starter, Pro or Team. See [Plans and credits](/ai-workforce/plans-and-credits).
- **A business and a project.** The first-run setup asks for both; a business can hold more than one project.
- **Nothing to install.** Workser runs in your browser. The desktop app is separate and covers Workser Code.
