## Prohibited activities

You may not use Workser to:

- Violate applicable law, or infringe anyone's intellectual property, privacy, or other legal rights.
- Attempt to bypass, disable, or interfere with security controls, sandbox isolation, rate limits, or access-control mechanisms.
- Use an AI agent to attempt prompt injection, data exfiltration, or to access systems, accounts, or data you don't own or haven't been authorized to access.
- Use computer-use or Orbit capabilities on a device or account you aren't authorized to control.
- Send unsolicited bulk communications, or use connected integrations to do so on a third party's behalf without their consent.
- Generate or distribute malware, or use the Services to conduct attacks against other systems.
- Misrepresent your identity or affiliation.
- Resell or provide the Services to third parties without our prior written consent, except as expressly permitted by your plan.
- Systematically extract our AI agent orchestration, sandboxing, or workflow engine implementation to build a competing product.

## AI-specific use restrictions

Don't direct an AI Employee or computer-use agent to take an action you wouldn't take yourself with the same authorization. Don't grant an agent access to systems or data where an error would cause harm you're not prepared to accept, and review computer-use approval prompts before confirming them.

## Reporting a violation

Contact [support@workser.ai](mailto:support@workser.ai).

## Enforcement

Violating this policy may result in content removal, suspension, or termination of your account.
