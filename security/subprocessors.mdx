A subprocessor is any third party that processes your data on our behalf. We keep this list current — if you're an enterprise customer with a notice requirement for new subprocessors in your DPA, we'll notify you per that agreement before a new one goes live.

## AI model providers

| Subprocessor | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Anthropic | LLM inference for AI agents/employees |
| OpenAI | LLM inference |
| Google (Generative AI / Gemini) | LLM inference, image generation |
| Others (OpenRouter, xAI, DeepSeek, and additional model routes) | LLM inference, depending on model selection |

Which providers are active for your account depends on the model selected in your workflow/agent configuration — not every provider processes every customer's data.
