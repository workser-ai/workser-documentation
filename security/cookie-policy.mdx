## Categories of cookies we use

| Category | Examples | Can you disable it? |
|---|---|---|
| Strictly necessary | Authentication session, CSRF protection | No — required for the Services to function |
| Analytics | PostHog (product usage analytics) | Yes, where required by law |
| Advertising | Google Ads conversion tracking, X (Twitter) Ads conversion tracking | Yes, where required by law |
| Payments | Stripe (fraud prevention on checkout pages) | No, on pages where you're completing a payment |

## How to control cookies

Most browsers let you block or delete cookies in their settings. Blocking strictly necessary cookies will prevent you from signing in or using the app. Blocking analytics and advertising cookies doesn't affect core functionality.
