Orbit is the part of [Workser Desktop](/workser-desktop/overview) that turns an AI agent's work into **software you can actually use**.

You describe what you need. The AI agent you already pay for writes it. Orbit gives that app everywhere it needs to live: a database of its own, sign-in for your users, file storage, a web address, and a Preview you check before anything goes public.

## Who this is for

Orbit was built for the person who has an idea and an AI subscription, and no interest in becoming a cloud engineer to get from one to the other.

- **You don't need to write code.** Describe the thing in your own words; your agent writes it.
- **You don't need a second AI bill.** The intelligence comes from the subscription you already pay for.
- **You don't need to pick a database, a host, or a domain registrar.** Every Orbit project arrives with those already switched on.

If you *are* a developer, none of that gets in your way — the same project is a normal repository your agent works in, plus a [command-line tool](/orbit/cli) and an [SDK](/orbit/sdk) it can drive directly.

## What Orbit is not

<Warning>
Orbit is **not** [Computer Use](/workser-desktop/computer-use). They ship in the same desktop app and share your account, but they do different jobs and neither requires the other.

- **Computer Use** is Workser's own hosted agent that sees your screen and controls your mouse, keyboard and files.
- **Orbit** is a bridge between *your own* coding agent and Workser's app infrastructure.

You can't swap your local CLI into Computer Use, and Orbit doesn't drive your screen.
</Warning>

## How the pieces fit

| Piece | What it is | Where it runs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Your AI agent** | Claude Code, Codex or OpenCode | Your machine, your subscription |
| **Orbit** | The workspace, Preview, Publish, and project memory | Workser Desktop on your machine |
| **The Orbit daemon** | A small local service your agent talks to | `127.0.0.1` only, never exposed |
| **`workser` CLI** | The commands your agent runs to ship and operate the app | Your machine, gated by Orbit |
| **`@workser/app` SDK** | How the finished app reads its own data at runtime | Inside your deployed app |
| **Workser cloud** | Database, hosting, storage, auth, domains | Workser's infrastructure |

Your prompts, your agent's reasoning, and the code it writes stay between your machine and your agent's own provider. Only deploy-time artifacts reach Workser — the exact list is in [Security & approvals](/orbit/security-and-approvals).
