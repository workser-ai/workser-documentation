Four steps. The only prerequisite is an AI agent CLI you're already signed in to — or ten minutes to set up the free one.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Download Workser Desktop">
    Get the app for macOS from [workser.ai/orbit](https://workser.ai/orbit) and sign in with your Workser account. The download is free, and every new organization starts with **7 days of Spark free — no card required**.

    Full installer details, permissions and troubleshooting are in [Install & setup](/workser-desktop/install-setup).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Connect your AI agent">
    Open **Orbit** in the app. It scans your machine for supported agent CLIs and shows which ones are installed and signed in.

    Pick one and connect it. You authenticate through that agent's own login command in Orbit's terminal — Workser never sees those credentials.

    <Note>
    Don't pay for any AI yet? Choose **OpenCode** and Orbit will walk you onto a free model, so you can go all the way through this guide at no AI cost. See [Connect your agent](/orbit/connect-your-agent#the-free-path).
    </Note>

  </Step>

  <Step title="Describe what you want">
    Create an app and tell your agent what it should do, in plain language:

    > *"A booking page for my salon. Customers pick a service and a time, I see the day's bookings on one screen, and everyone gets an email confirmation."*

    Your agent writes the code. When it needs a database, sign-in, or file storage, it provisions them through Orbit — you'll see each of those steps happen, and you'll be asked to approve anything with a real-world consequence.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Preview, then Publish">
    Every change opens in a **private Preview** — a real, working version of the app that only you can see. Try it. Ask for a revision. Repeat.

    When it's right, press **Publish**. That's the only thing that puts it in front of other people.

## What to tell your agent

Orbit ships instructions your agent can read, so it knows the `workser` commands exist and when to use them. Inside Workser Desktop this is set up for you during onboarding.

If you're running the CLI on its own, install the instructions yourself — see [Teach your agent](/orbit/teach-your-agent).

## Habits that pay off early

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Ask for one thing at a time" icon="list-check">
    "Add the booking form" gets a better result than "build the whole salon system, and also a loyalty program". You can always ask for the next thing after you've previewed the last one.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Preview before you ask for the next change" icon="eye">
    It's much easier to say "the time slots should be 30 minutes, not 60" while you're looking at the thing than to describe it in advance.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Say no when an approval looks wrong" icon="hand">
    Orbit pauses and asks before anything with a real consequence — sending mail, changing data, running a command. If a request doesn't match what you asked for, decline it and ask your agent what it was trying to do.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Let it keep notes" icon="brain">
    Orbit keeps a board, docs, decisions and memory for each project. That's what lets your agent pick the project back up next week without re-deriving everything. See [The project workspace](/orbit/project-workspace).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
