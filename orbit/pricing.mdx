Orbit's pricing follows directly from how it works: Workser isn't running the AI, so Workser doesn't charge you for it.

## What you pay for, and to whom

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Your AI agent" icon="robot">
    Paid to Anthropic, OpenAI, or whichever provider your CLI uses — on the plan you already have. **Workser charges nothing for this.**
  </Card>
  <Card title="Your app's infrastructure" icon="server">
    Paid to Workser. A dedicated database, hosting, storage, sign-in, domains and deploys are real infrastructure to run.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## The free trial

**Every new organization starts with 7 days of Spark free — no card required.**

Downloading Workser Desktop is free, and so is connecting your agent. The trial covers everything Orbit does: build, preview, publish, a real database, a real web address.

After 7 days without a subscription, Orbit and other paid-tier features pause. **Nothing is deleted** — your project and its data stay exactly where they are; you just need an active subscription to keep deploying and running it.

<Note>
If you want to reach $0 total, connect **OpenCode** with a free model. Combined with the trial that's a genuinely free path from download to a published app. See [the free path](/orbit/connect-your-agent#the-free-path).
</Note>

## Spark — $10/month

Spark is the entry paid plan, and it's built deliberately around *your* agent subscription:

- Run your own local AI agent (Claude Code, Codex, OpenCode)
- A **dedicated Postgres database** for every project — not a shared schema
- Hosting, deploys, and a stable web address
- **Unlimited** end-user authentication — you're not billed per person who signs up for your app
- File storage and environment secrets
- **No monthly Workser AI credits** — stated as an explicit exclusion, not an omission

That last point is the whole design. The $10 covers the infrastructure, not the intelligence. Anyone comparing plans should be able to see that before subscribing, not discover it after.

<Info>
Project counts, app counts and storage quotas change as plans evolve. The [pricing page](https://workser.ai/pricing) is the current source of truth for exact limits — this page states the shape, not the numbers.
</Info>

## Higher tiers

Starter and Scale add **Workser AI credits** on top of everything Spark has — for when you also want Workser's own hosted agents (AI Employees, workflow automation, Computer Use) alongside your local CLI. They also raise project, app and storage limits.

See [Payments & Billing](/consumer/payments-billing) for the full ladder, and the [pricing page](https://workser.ai/pricing) for current numbers.

## Why bring your own key is cheaper

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="You're not paying twice for AI" icon="wallet">
    A vibe-coding platform runs its own model and bills you for inference behind every change, on top of your subscription to it — while you're *also* still paying for Claude or ChatGPT. Orbit runs no model on the code-generation path, so there's exactly one AI bill and it isn't Workser's.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Your prompts don't route through Workser's providers" icon="shield">
    Only deploy-time artifacts reach Workser. See [Security & approvals](/orbit/security-and-approvals#what-stays-local-what-goes-to-the-cloud).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="No lock-in on the agent itself" icon="unlock">
    Switch which CLI you use, or run several, without Workser being in the middle of that choice. Your project doesn't change.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I need a card to try Orbit?">
    No. The 7-day Spark trial every new organization gets requires no card.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens to my app when the trial ends?">
    Deploying and running it pause until you subscribe. Your project, code and data are kept — nothing is deleted.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Workser charge for the AI my agent uses?">
    Not on the Orbit path. Your agent runs on your own provider account. Workser AI credits only apply to Workser's *own* hosted agents, which Spark deliberately doesn't include.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Am I billed per user of the app I build?">
    No. End-user authentication is unlimited on every plan.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
