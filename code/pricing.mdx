**Workser Code is included in every Workser plan.** One plan covers your AI workforce, Workser Code and the hosting your apps run on. The full plan comparison, add-ons and charging rules are in [Plans, credits and add-ons](/ai-workforce/plans-and-credits); this page covers what is specific to building and hosting apps.

Every paid project has its own dedicated Postgres database. Multiple apps in a project share that database and its file storage. Need more projects? Add them for $3 a month each; each extra project adds one more free hosting base. Domain registration is bought separately, and you configure the domain's DNS records.

Every plan is paid; there is no free plan. Paid plans have a 7-day money-back guarantee: your plan price back, minus credits you used.

**Spark** ($10 a month, 800 credits) is sold only in the desktop app, and current Spark customers keep it.

**Enterprise** has custom pricing, capacity and credit terms. [Talk to us](https://workser.ai/contact).

## Hosting past the free base

Each project with a live app gets a free hosting base every month: **0.5 GB database, 5 compute hours, 10 GB files, 2 GB transfer and 500 workflow runs**, up to your plan's number of bases (Starter 1, Pro 3, Team 1) plus one for each extra project you buy. The bases are added together, so three app projects on Pro share 1.5 GB of database, 15 compute hours, 30 GB of files, 6 GB of transfer and 1,500 workflow runs. Every paid plan gets at least one base, even before an app goes live.

Past the free base, hosting goes on your plan's monthly invoice, never your credits, so your credits stay for your AI agents:

| Cloud resource | Price |
| --- | --- |
| Database storage | $2 per GB a month |
| Database compute | $0.40 per hour |
| File storage | $0.10 per GB a month |
| Data transfer (egress) | $0.60 per GB |
| Workflow runs | $1 per 1,000 runs |

For example, 10 GB of transfer past the free base is $6 on your next invoice. If hosting passes $20 in a month on Starter, $100 on Pro or $20 per person on Team, we email the owners; your apps keep running.

Project, app and domain limits need a plan upgrade or extra projects rather than credits.

## Workser Code credits

### Connected coding agents

Claude Code, Codex, Cursor Agent, GitHub Copilot and OpenCode use the account configured in their own CLI. Those model calls do **not** spend Workser credits. Their provider can still charge you or apply subscription limits.

### The built-in Workser Code agent

You do not need a separate AI subscription to use the built-in agent. Its model usage is paid from your organization's credits:

- Each model call is charged the moment it finishes, in the units the provider returns.
- A live meter shows the credits a task uses as it runs.
- A task that fails pays for what actually ran.
- Plan credits refresh every month and unused ones expire. Credits you buy never expire: 1,000 for $10.

Every plan can choose premium AI models.

## Agent Cloud (Beta) and other hosted AI

[Agent Cloud](/code/agent-cloud) runs hosted agents on Workser infrastructure. Their model usage, sandbox and runtime are charged in credits as they run, separately from any connected coding-agent subscription.
