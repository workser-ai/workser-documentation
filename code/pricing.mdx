The **Workser Code desktop app is free to download**. Your Workser plan covers the workspace and cloud services that run your software. Connected coding agents use their own provider account; the built-in Workser Code agent uses Workser credits.

## The free trial

New organizations start with **7 days of Spark free. No card required.** Choose a paid plan to continue using paid cloud features after the trial. Trial expiry does not itself delete your project or its data; it is not a promise of indefinite retention.

Spark has no monthly model credit grant. Use a connected coding agent or purchase credits for the built-in agent. A third-party free model is subject to that provider's availability and limits.

## Compare plans

These are the published self-service plans as of **September 6, 2026**. Allowances belong to your organization. Existing subscriptions and Enterprise agreements can differ; check your organization's Billing view before changing plans.

| Included | Spark | Starter | Scale |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Monthly price, USD | **$10** | **$25** | **$100** |
| Workser Code credits per month | None; top-ups available | 1,500 | 6,500 |
| Projects | 2 | 4 | 20 |
| Apps per project | 5 | 10 | 10 web apps |
| Database storage | 1 GB | 8 GB | 40 GB |
| File storage | 5 GB | 100 GB | 500 GB |
| Data transfer (egress) per month | 10 GB | 100 GB | 500 GB |
| Custom domains | Included | 3 | Unlimited |
| App user authentication | Unlimited users | Unlimited users | Unlimited users |
| Workspace team members | 1 | Up to 3 | Up to 10 |
| Monthly workflow executions | No allowance listed | 5,000 | 80,000 |
| Backups | No daily backup feature listed | Automatic daily backups | Daily backups with point-in-time recovery |
| Support | Community | Priority email | Priority support |

Each paid project has its own Postgres database. Multiple apps in a project share that database and its file storage. The storage allowances above are pooled across the organization's projects, not multiplied by the project count.

**Custom domains are available on Spark.** Domain registration is purchased separately, and you must configure the domain's DNS records. App users are the people signing into your software; workspace team members are the people managing projects with you.

**Enterprise** has custom pricing, cloud allowances and credit terms. SSO, dedicated infrastructure, data residency, an SLA and a named customer success manager are scoped in the agreement. [Contact sales](https://workser.ai/contact).

Free accounts and existing Launch subscriptions are not additional current self-service plans. See your Billing view for legacy subscription terms.

## Cloud overage

Your included usage comes first. Published overage rates apply to usage above that allowance, in addition to the monthly plan:

| Cloud resource | Published overage rate |
| --- | --- |
| Database storage | **$1.25 per additional GB** |
| File storage | **$0.15 per additional GB** |
| Data transfer (egress) | **$0.50 per additional GB** |

For example, **10 GB of egress above your monthly allowance costs $5** at $0.50 per GB. Egress is data transferred out of your apps and files.

<Note>
Overage billing depends on the usage meters enabled for your organization. A published rate alone does not confirm that automatic charging is active. Check Billing or [contact support](https://workser.ai/contact) to confirm how excess usage is handled for your account.
</Note>

Project, app and team member limits require a plan upgrade rather than a per-GB charge. Cloud overage is separate from model credits. Enterprise overage follows your agreement.

## Workser Code credits

### Connected coding agents

Claude Code, Codex, Cursor Agent, GitHub Copilot and OpenCode use the account configured in their own CLI. Those model calls do **not** spend Workser Code credits. Their provider can still charge you or apply subscription limits.

### The built-in Workser Code agent

You do not need a separate AI subscription to use this agent. Its model usage is paid from your organization's credit balance. The selected model and the amount of work determine the cost.

- **100 credits equal $1.**
- The current conversion uses reported model cost plus **15%**, rounded to whole credits at settlement.
- A turn with **$1 of reported model cost uses 115 credits**.
- A turn is not a fixed-price step. Longer context, additional model calls and a different model can change its cost.
- The old weekly cap is no longer the model. Available credits determine whether a self-service run can start.

A balance check happens before a run; actual usage is settled afterward. A turn can finish with a negative balance when its final cost exceeds the balance available at the start. Top up before starting more paid work. Enterprise usage follows its agreement.

Starter includes 1,500 credits per month and Scale includes 6,500. Spark includes none, but supports purchased credits. A feature being available does not mean its model usage is unlimited or free.

## Agent Cloud and other hosted AI

[Agent Cloud](/code/agent-cloud) runs agents inside your software on Workser infrastructure. Its runtime, workspace and model usage are separate from your local coding-agent subscription. Workser Business AI Employees and Computer Use also have their own usage rules. The built-in coding agent's cost example above is not a rate card for all hosted AI services.
