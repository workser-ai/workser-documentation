**Workser is a proactive AI workforce that runs your business.** You hire AI agents by role; each one can have its own work email, phone number and logins, uses the project's shared cloud computer, works on its own and asks you only when a step is risky. It runs in your web browser, and plans start at $20 a month with a 7-day money-back guarantee.
