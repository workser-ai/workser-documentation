## What is Workser

Workser gives your business real AI agents — ones that can build software, run workflows, and operate your tools — with the security, access control, and data ownership guarantees an enterprise team needs, and the simplicity a solo builder wants.

Workser ships as two products on shared infrastructure:

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Workser Business" icon="browser" href="/workser-business/overview">
    The web platform. Build web apps from a prompt, automate workflows across your tools, and delegate real business functions to AI Employees.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Workser Desktop" icon="desktop" href="/workser-desktop/overview">
    A desktop AI agent with computer-use control (screen, mouse, keyboard, files) and Orbit — a bring-your-own-key bridge from your own coding-agent CLI to Workser's deploy infrastructure.
  </Card>
</Columns>
