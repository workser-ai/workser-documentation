Workser Desktop gives an AI agent hands-on control of your computer, and bundles **Orbit**, a separate tool that connects a coding-agent CLI you already run to Workser's deploy/hosting infrastructure. These are two distinct subsystems — don't confuse them.

## What stays local vs. what goes to the cloud

| Stays local | Goes to Workser's cloud |
|---|---|
| Encrypted credential store (your OS keychain) | Screenshots and action results during a computer-use session |
| Activity/bridge logs | Deploy, git, environment variable, domain, and billing calls (Orbit) |
| Orbit session handshake | — |
