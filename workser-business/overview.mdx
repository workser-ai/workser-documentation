## How a project is structured

Each project you create gets:
- Its own database (dedicated on paid plans — see [Security & Trust](/security/whitepaper) for the tenant isolation model).
- Its own file storage.
- Its own deployment/domain.
- Its own credit usage tracked separately, rolling up to your organization's shared credit wallet.

## Organizations and teams

Projects live inside an **organization**. Members get one of four roles — `owner`, `admin`, `member`, `viewer` — which determine what they can configure, including whether they can delete a project or manage billing. See [Enterprise vs. Consumer](/enterprise-vs-consumer) for how this scales for multi-seat teams.
