## 1. Create an account

Sign up at [workser.ai](https://workser.ai) with email/password or Google sign-in. If you were invited to an existing organization, follow the invite link instead — you'll join with the role your admin assigned you.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/workser-business/quickstart/workser-landing.png" alt="The Workser landing page" />
</Frame>

## 2. Create your first project

From your dashboard, start a new project and describe what you want to build in plain language — a landing page, an internal tool, a customer-facing app. Workser generates a working app inside a project scoped just to that build: its own database, its own file storage.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/workser-business/quickstart/prompt-step.png" alt="Describing a web app to build in Workser" />
</Frame>

## 3. Iterate

Keep prompting to add features, fix issues, or restyle. Each change applies to the same project — you're not starting over each time.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/workser-business/quickstart/iterate-step.png" alt="Iterating on a Workser project with follow-up prompts" />
</Frame>

## 4. Connect the tools you need

If your app or an agent needs to reach a third-party service (Gmail, Slack, GitHub, and 100+ others), go to **Connections** and authorize it once. Every workflow or AI Employee in your organization can then use that connection, scoped to what you approved. See [Connections & Integrations](/workser-business/connections-integrations).

## 5. Automate something

Once your app exists, add a [workflow](/workser-business/workflow-automation) to react to events (a webhook, a schedule, an app event) — or configure an [AI Employee](/workser-business/ai-employees) to handle an ongoing task on your behalf.

## 6. Deploy

Publish to a Workser-managed domain, or connect your own. Your project's database and storage travel with the deployment automatically.
