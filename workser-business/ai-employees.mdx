## How execution works

An AI Employee's task runs inside an isolated environment. When it needs to write or run code, that happens in an ephemeral sandbox created just for that task and destroyed when the task ends — never reused across customers or tasks. The agent draws on your organization's data (projects, connected apps, [Business Data Platform](/consumer/business-data-platform) records) rather than holding its own separate copy.

## Why this is safe to delegate real work to

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Sandbox isolation">
    Code execution happens in a disposable, per-task sandbox — not shared infrastructure.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Secret redaction">
    Any secret-shaped value in sandbox output is automatically masked before the model sees it — even if you explicitly ask for it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Prompt-injection guardrails">
    Agents are instructed to treat uploaded files and tool output as untrusted data, not instructions, and to refuse embedded attempts to change their scope.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Human-in-the-loop">
    An agent can pause and request your approval before proceeding on a sensitive step (default wait window: 24 hours).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

See the full [Security Whitepaper](/security/whitepaper#ai-agent-safety-controls) for the complete control list — including the honest gap: human approval today is agent-invoked (voluntary), not a platform-enforced mandatory gate on a specific list of high-risk actions. If your organization requires mandatory approval on categories like outbound payments, confirm this with your Workser contact rather than assuming it's the default.

## Training use

Your prompts, files, and the outputs your AI Employees generate are **not** used to train Workser's models or shared with any other customer, without your written consent. See our [Privacy Policy](/security/privacy-policy).

## Getting the most from an AI Employee

Give it real context — connect the apps it needs, and fill out your organization's [Business Data Platform](/consumer/business-data-platform) so it reasons about your actual company and customers instead of generic assumptions.
