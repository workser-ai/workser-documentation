## 7-day money-back guarantee

7-day money-back guarantee: your plan price back, minus credits you used. Within 7 days of your first plan payment, an owner or admin can ask from Settings, Plan. Credits you used are valued at your plan's price; your plan, its credits and add-ons end. Once per business.

## Credits

- 100 credits = $1 when you pay.
- Your plan's credits refresh every month; unused ones expire.
- Credits you buy never expire: **1,000 credits for $10**.
- Past both, work keeps going as overage on your next bill, up to a limit.

## How charging works

- **AI calls:** each model call is charged the moment it finishes, using the units the provider returns: input and output tokens.
- **Infrastructure:** cloud computer and sandbox minutes, the cloud browser, phone and email are charged when each piece finishes or fails, whether it worked or not.
- **Failed tasks** pay for what actually ran, no more, and their credits are not given back.
- **Before and during a task:** a live meter shows the credits a task uses as it runs. Once similar tasks have finished, you also see an estimate before it starts, and a task that passes its estimate asks once whether to keep going or stop.
- **Credits and usage** shows every charge with its units and price.

## Task spending limit

**No single task can quietly use up your month.** A task pauses and asks you once it has used **10% of your monthly credits**:

| Plan | A task pauses at |
| --- | --- |
| Free | 5 credits |
| Starter | 180 credits |
| Pro | 1,000 credits |

You choose:

- **Keep going:** it may use the same amount again, then asks again.
- **Stop the Task:** it stops, and you pay only for what it already used.

With no answer in 4 hours, the task stops.

To set your own limit for an AI agent, open **Settings**, then **AI workforce**, press **Manage** on the AI agent and set **Credit limit per task** under Advanced. A task with its own limit stops when it reaches it, without asking. Leave it blank for the 10% pause.

Upgrades, invoices and cancelling are on [Your plan and account](/ai-workforce/plan-and-account).

## Cloud computer time

Cloud computer time is paid from your credits on every plan: **25 credits an hour, billed per second**. No plan includes free hours.

## Hosting for apps you build

Apps built with [Workser Code](/code/overview) get one free hosting base on every paid plan, per organization per month: 0.5 GB database, 5 compute hours, 10 GB files, 2 GB transfer and 500 workflow runs. Past that, hosting is paid in credits:

| Resource | Credits |
| --- | --- |

| Database compute | 40 per hour |
| File storage | 10 per GB a month |
| Data transfer | 60 per GB |
| Workflow runs | 100 per 1,000 runs |

No. Workser runs the AI for you. Everything is paid in credits, so there is one bill and no keys to manage.
