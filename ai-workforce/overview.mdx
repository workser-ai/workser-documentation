## What is an AI workforce?

An AI workforce is a team of AI agents that each own a job in your business, like answering customers, checking invoices or writing posts, and do it on their own. Unlike a chatbot, each one has its own logins and a shared cloud computer, keeps working while you're away, and asks you only when a step is risky.

## How it differs from a chatbot, an AI chat app and automation

These are kinds of tools, not products. Many products mix them, and a simple automation is often the cheapest choice for a fixed task.

| | Chatbot | AI chat app | Automation tool | AI workforce |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Who starts the work | A customer, with a question | You, each time | A trigger you set up | You, an Automation, or the AI agent itself |
| What it does | Answers from a script or a help center | Writes and answers in a chat window | Runs the same steps every time | Does whole tasks in your apps, start to finish |
| Where it works | On your website | In a chat window | Between connected apps | A shared cloud computer, a web browser and your apps |
| When something new comes up | Hands over to a person | Waits for your next message | Stops until someone fixes it | Works it out, or asks you if it's risky |
| Best for | Common customer questions | Thinking and drafting with you | Fixed, repeated steps | Ongoing work you'd give a new hire |

## Who it's for

- Owners of small businesses who do the desk work themselves after hours.
- Solo founders and freelancers who want admin, inbox and follow-ups off their plate.
- Small teams that want routine work done the same way every day without hiring for it yet.

Read [AI agents by type of business](/ai-workforce/use-cases): online shops, SaaS, freelancers, restaurants, agencies and local services.

## What it isn't

- It doesn't replace your judgment: risky steps come to you, and you can stop any task.
- It works best on tasks you can describe in plain words and check afterwards.
- Work phone numbers are US and Canada only today; elsewhere it works by email and chat apps.
