## When Workser fits

- You want a **team** hired by role, not one general helper: 18 ready-made AI agents and 5 teams, from Customer Support to Bookkeeper.
- You want them to work in **your apps** on a shared cloud computer and a cloud browser that stays signed in, while your laptop is closed.
- You want them to **ask only when it's risky**: refunds, deletes, cancellations, payments from $50 and emails to new contacts.
- You want to **pay for the work, not seats**: free to start, then from $20 a month, with a 7-day money-back guarantee.

## Workser's limits today

- Work phone numbers are US and Canada only; elsewhere AI agents work by email and chat apps.
- Workser is new, so treat it as beta. See [Compliance status](/security/compliance-status) for what is and isn't certified.
