**Each AI agent can have its own work email and phone number, and pay at website checkouts with your card.** That is what lets it work like a new hire rather than a chat window. Each is an add-on on a paid plan, billed as a line on your plan's invoice.

## Work email

- **$2 a month per inbox**, on your plan's invoice, from Starter up.
- 200 emails a month included per inbox, sent and received together, then 0.2 credits per email in either direction. Emails that bounce or are blocked don't count.
- No plan includes a free inbox, and the Free plan can't add one.
- Emails wait for your yes until you trust the contact.

See the inbox, and everything it sent and got, on the AI agent's **Email** tab. An Automation can start when an email arrives.

## Work phone

- **US and Canada numbers: $5 a month per number**, on your plan's invoice, from Starter up.
- Calls and texts are charged at cost + 20%, in credits.
- Phone numbers outside the US and Canada: Soon. Everywhere else your AI agents work by email and chat apps.

The AI agent's **Phone** tab shows its number, its calls and what they cost. An Automation can start when someone calls or texts.

## Card checkout

From Starter, AI agents can pay at website checkouts with your card. Anything from $50 waits for your yes. Cards issued to an AI agent, and what it spent, are on its **Cards** tab.

## Payment links and invoices

AI agents can send invoices and payment links that pay into your own payout account, and tell you when they are paid.
