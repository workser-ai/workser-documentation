**Invite people by email from Settings › People.** Everyone in your business sees and works in every project, with the same AI agents, Automations, Logins and Company knowledge. Nothing has to be set up twice.

## Invite someone

1. Open **Settings › People**.
2. Under **Invite someone**, type their email.
3. Choose what they **Can** do: **Member**, **Admin** or **View only**.
4. Press **Invite**.

- **If they already have a Workser account,** they join at once and get an email saying they've been added.
- **If they don't,** they join when they sign up with that email.

Until then they show as **Invited**, with a **Cancel** button.

## What each person can do

| | Owner | Admin | Member | View only |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Invite and remove people | Yes | Yes | No | No |
| Change someone's role | Yes | Yes | No | No |
| Change the plan, billing and refunds | Yes | Yes | No | No |
| Rename or delete a project | Yes | Yes | No | No |
| See credits and usage | Yes | Yes | No | No |

Each business has one owner, the person who created it. The owner's own role can't be changed from People; ownership transfers are handled by Workser.

**View only** is for someone who should read rather than change the work. Record-level access is set separately in **Business › Who sees what**.

## Change someone's role

In **People**, each member row has their role (Member, Admin or View only). Pick another and it saves at once; the note confirms the new role. You can't change your own role or the owner's.

## Remove someone

In **People**, press **Remove** next to the person and confirm. They lose access to the business at once.

## Which plan for a team

Starter and Pro are priced for one person. **Team** is $20 per person a month (2 people minimum), with 2,200 credits per person shared by everyone, one shared task board, and AI agents, skills and connectors set up once for the whole team. See [Plans and credits](/ai-workforce/plans-and-credits).
