**Workser can help you roll AI agents out across your team.** You can set Workser up on your own, and most businesses do. This is for organizations that want a plan, training or a faster security review.

## What you can get

| | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **Implementation consulting** | We map your workflows, tools and processes onto Workser, review how it connects to your apps, and agree a rollout plan for your organization. |
| **Team training** | Hands-on workshops on giving AI agents work, setting up [Automations](/ai-workforce/automations) and running Workser safely, for the people who will use it, such as ops, engineering or IT and security. |
| **Security walkthrough** | A guided session through the [Security whitepaper](/security/whitepaper), how your data is handled and the [current compliance status](/security/compliance-status), with your security or procurement team. |

## Ask for help

- **Send a request:** fill in the form at [workser.ai/consulting](https://workser.ai/consulting) with your name, work email, company, team size and what you want to do. The team replies within a couple of business days.
- **Book a call:** the same page has a calendar to book time straight away.
