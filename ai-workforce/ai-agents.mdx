Workser has **18 ready-made AI agents and 5 teams** you can hire in a minute, or you can create your own. They share a cloud computer, use your apps and ask you before risky steps. Every plan comes with **Workser**, who leads your team and hands tasks to the right AI agent.

## What every AI agent gets

- A shared cloud computer that keeps working when your laptop is closed, and a cloud browser that stays signed in.
- Your saved logins, filled in by name: passwords are encrypted and the AI agent never sees them.
- Its own work email and phone number if you add them (email $2 a month per inbox; US and Canada numbers only today). See [Identity](/ai-workforce/identity).
- It asks before risky steps: refunds, deletes and cancellations, payments from $50, and emails to people you haven't trusted yet.
- It texts you in WhatsApp, Telegram, Discord or LINE when a task is done or stuck, and every finished task is checked before it says Done.

## Teams

A team hires several AI agents that work together on one kind of work. Each member asks before its own risky steps.

| Team | Members | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Customer care | Customer Support, Operations Coordinator, Sales Rep | Answers customers, keeps orders moving and follows up leads. |
| Marketing and growth | Marketing Manager, Writer, Graphic Designer, Video Editor | Plans campaigns, writes the words and makes the visuals and videos. |
| Research and reports | Researcher, Data Analyst, Presentation Maker | Turns questions and data into a sourced report and a deck. |
| Inbox and admin | Inbox Manager, Researcher, Operations Coordinator | Sorts your inbox, looks things up and keeps daily work on track. |
| Website or app | App & Website Designer, Software Developer, Computer Operator | Designs, builds and tests your website or app. |

## Roles

Example tasks are tasks you can give it, not results we promise.

### All-rounder

Everyday tasks: research, documents, slides and your apps.

- **Example tasks:** Research three suppliers and put their prices in a sheet; Turn my notes into a one-page proposal; Update the opening hours on my listings.
- **Asks you first:** Asks before payments from $50, deletes, and emails to people you haven't trusted yet.
- [See All-rounder on workser.ai](https://workser.ai/ai-agents#general)

### Customer Support

Answers customer questions and drafts replies and help articles.

- **Example tasks:** Answer this week's customer emails about delivery times; Draft replies to new reviews for my OK; Write help articles for the ten questions we get most.
- **Asks you first:** Asks before refunds and cancellations. Emails wait for your yes until you trust the contact.
- [See Customer Support on workser.ai](https://workser.ai/ai-agents#support)

### Sales Rep

Finds leads, keeps your contacts tidy and drafts outreach for your OK.

- **Example tasks:** Find 30 local cafés that might stock our coffee; Tidy my contact list and fill in missing company names; Draft a follow-up to everyone who asked for a quote last month.
- **Asks you first:** Drafts outreach for your OK: the first email to each new lead waits for your yes.
- [See Sales Rep on workser.ai](https://workser.ai/ai-agents#sales)

### Marketing Manager

Plans campaigns, ads, email and SEO, and reports what worked.

- **Example tasks:** Plan next month's email and social calendar; Write three versions of an ad for our spring sale; Report which posts brought the most visits last month.
- **Asks you first:** Asks before spending from $50 and before emailing contacts you haven't trusted yet.
- [See Marketing Manager on workser.ai](https://workser.ai/ai-agents#marketing)

### Writer

Blogs, newsletters, website copy, posts and scripts in your voice.

- **Example tasks:** Write a blog post about our new menu in our voice; Turn this product list into website copy; Draft this month's newsletter.
- **Asks you first:** Asks before sending anything to people you haven't trusted yet.
- [See Writer on workser.ai](https://workser.ai/ai-agents#writer)

### Graphic Designer

### Software Developer

Builds and fixes websites, apps and the code behind them.
